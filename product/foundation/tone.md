# Tone

**The direction (the PM, 2026-10-05):** "a subtle california cruisey language vibe around here." The California way of seeing it: **work to live, not live to work.**

`voice.md` is the voice: warm, plainspoken, a little wry, never corporate. This file is the weather that voice lives in. Where they differ, `voice.md` wins.

## The feel

- **Unhurried.** Nothing here is urgent. No countdowns, no "don't miss out", no pressure to keep up.
- **Sunny, not loud.** Friendly and easy. One light touch per screen at most, and most screens need none.
- **Life first.** Time off, weekends, evenings, the people you like. Work, errands and admin are what you get through to get there.
- **Outside and in person.** The point is to get up and go: a farmers market, a river float, a pickup game, a class on a Saturday morning.
- **Laid back, still clear.** Easy does not mean vague. Every line still says plainly what happens next.

## How it sounds

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Don't miss out! Events are filling up fast. | Here's what's on this weekend. |
| Complete your profile to get started. | Add a picture when you get a minute. |
| You have 3 pending actions. | A few things are waiting when you're ready. |
| Submit | Post it |
| Error: request failed. | That didn't go through. Mind trying again? |
| Maximize your reach. | Let people know it's on. |
| Nothing found. | Nothing here yet. Maybe try a bit wider. |

Lines that fit:

- "Make some time for it."
- "Plenty going on. No rush."
- "See you out there."
- "Work can wait. This is the fun part."
- "Be creative. Start anything that helps you find your people."

## Keep it subtle

- **No caricature.** No "dude", "gnarly", "stoked", surf puns or emoji palm trees. It should feel like a relaxed neighbor, not a costume.
- **Local, not generic.** Sacramento is river towns, delta breezes, farm to fork and long summer evenings, not beaches and Hollywood. Name real things when they fit; don't force them.
- **One flourish, then out.** If a screen already has a friendly line, the rest stays plain.
- **Never at the cost of clarity.** Errors, safety, reports, legal and money stay plain and direct. The ease is in the welcome, the empty states and the nudges, not in the rules.
- **No hustle language.** No "grow", "scale", "level up", "crush it", "grind" or "productivity". People come here to live a little.

## Where it shows most

1. Welcome and onboarding
2. Empty states
3. Confirmations and toasts
4. The weekend and evening rows on Explore
5. Create, including "Be creative"

## Where it stays out

Reports and moderation, Terms and Privacy, sign-in errors, and anything about someone's safety or account.

## Mechanics

Everything in `voice.md` still applies: no em dashes, no "not just X, but Y", no people as categories, no zero counts on your own work, and plainlanguage.gov for the basics.
