# Member journey

**What changed (2026-10-05):** each loop now names its Page purpose, the Pages, Posts and follows it runs on today, and its status from `ROADMAP.md`; the maker and provider "profiles" are Pages, since members have no type and no public profile.

Foundational north star, upstream of every system spec. The platform exists so people can take ownership of their economic and civic future together — not by being an online third place, but by making real-world third places findable, recurring, and economically consequential. It is deliberately not built to grow like Facebook: as communities mature, deeper infrastructure (banking, insurance, shared services) spawns into separate federated platforms rather than being absorbed. This platform stays the foundational coordination layer, indefinitely.

## How the loops are carried today

- **A Page is what anyone makes.** Its **purpose** is chosen first: **Gather**, **Sell**, **Offer a service or teach**, or **Be creative** (Don, 2026-10-05). Business or Social group is worked out from the purpose for listing, never picked as an identity.
- **A member has no type and no public profile** (2026-10-01). Someone who wants to be found or followed — an artist, a plumber, a run club — makes a Page. You is private.
- **A Post is what a Page says** — an update, or a dated one with a time and a place (the user-facing noun since 2026-10-05).
- **Follow** is a member's link to a Page. Delivering a Page's posts to its followers is cut for beta; followed Pages' posts reach a signed-in reader on Explore instead (F059 criterion 2b).
- **Status** is `ROADMAP.md`'s: **beta** (in for 2026-10-30), **later** (wanted, after beta), **cut** (taken off the beta list, may return).

## The loops, in order

Five families, each a deeper level of activation energy and stake. Members enter through the lightest loops; nobody is required to traverse the full sequence.

### Family 1 — Gathering (the civic on-ramp)

The lightest, most universal loops — no belief required, only the desire to be less alone in a place.

1. **Find your people.** A recurring real-world gathering (a run club, a chess meetup) is findable only by being there and asking. **Today:** a Gather Page, found on Explore and the map, with one shareable URL; following it is how a member keeps it. **Beta.**
2. **Float an idea.** Someone's thinking of starting something but doesn't know if anyone would come. Wonder — one sentence, no commitment; interest tells them whether to start it, and they make the Page themselves (Loop 4). **Nothing converts** *(2026-09-15)*. **Later:** the substrate shipped, the composer and its page did not.
3. **Land here.** A newcomer wants to know what's happening within walking distance this week. **Today:** signed out, a visitor sees each Page's front door — name, picture, description — with its posts withheld behind sign-in (F093); signed in, What's happening by date. Newcomers are the highest-intent people this platform serves. **Beta, partial:** the no-login view shows Pages, not posts.
4. **Gather regularly.** An organizer already convening something wants it findable and persistent. **Today:** a Gather Page anchored to where it meets, with dated Posts and a weekly repeating series (F074) feeding What's happening (F091). **Beta.** Saying you're going (RSVP) is **cut** for beta.

### Family 2 — Sharing (mutual aid)

No money changes hands; neighbors start recognizing each other as resources.

5. **Share what you have.** Extra zucchini, a tool to lend, an hour to teach. Offer ("Offer up"), the mutual-aid half of the volunteering pair. **Later:** blocked on member-to-member messaging, not on the composer. Teaching for pay or regularly is already a Page (Offer a service or teach).
6. **Ask for help.** A truck for a move, a babysitter recommendation. Ask, same reach as Offer. **Later**, for the same reason.

### Family 3 — Trade (the economic loop)

The substance of the platform — where participation becomes local economic strength.

7. **Make and be found.** A maker wants to be findable by neighbors who'd buy from them, beyond one market day. **Today:** a Page — purpose Sell, or Be creative for an artist — anchored to where they are or the markets they're usually at, with a picture and Posts. **Beta.** Individual product listings on a Page are **later** (postponed 2026-10-01).
8. **Follow what you love.** A buyer wants to find a good maker again. Follow — the one early signal that matters: do people come back? **Today:** follow a Page; its posts show on Explore for a signed-in follower. **Beta.** Delivering posts to followers (Bulletins) is **cut**.
9. **Find a local pro.** A plumber, a vet, an accountant. Existing tools (Yelp, Angi) charge for visibility and gate trust behind star ratings that hurt small operators. **Today:** a Page with purpose Offer a service or teach, its phone and where it works (2026-09-30: neighbourhoods as plain text). **Beta.** Vouching (F095) and a quiet Saved list are **later**; search over Pages is **cut** for beta. *Test every service-provider proposal against: would the trusted-neighbor pattern still operate under this?*

### Family 4 — Pooling (community ownership)

Members become stakeholders; capital is pooled, businesses founded, resources shared. Ownership concentration is the squeeze; collective ownership is the answer (the wealth-circulation absolute lives in `goals.md`). **The app is a finding mechanism** (2026-09-19): the organizing happens in real life, and where it needs a legal form, in entities formed outside the platform.

10. **Start something.** An idea that would grow the community's capacity. **Reshaped 2026-09-19:** there is no goal or Initiative object; starting something is making a Page and posting from it, and Wonder (Loop 2) tests interest first. Encouragement and Pledge have no design. **Later.**
11. **Pool resources.** Members combine capital for something no household could acquire alone — land, a building, a workshop's tools. Pledges hand off to partner CDFIs for the transaction; the platform holds no funds for itself (Won't). **Later, far horizon.**
12. **Steward what we built.** Shared ownership needs ongoing care — a maintenance schedule, a workday rotation. **Today:** stewarding a Page is its managing role, which every non-business Page already has. **Beta** for the role; shared schedules and inventory are **later**. Shared resources fail more often from atrophied stewardship than external pressure.

### Family 5 — Federation (the spawn boundary)

13. **Federate and spawn.** A community's needs exceed what this platform should try to do — real banking, shared bookkeeping and insurance, regional intelligence. The handoff to dedicated, separate, federated platforms, connected through identity and protocol, never absorbed. This is the platform's most important architectural commitment and the answer to "what stops this from becoming Facebook" — a platform trying to do banking, insurance, and gathering coordination all at once fails at all of them. **Later, far horizon.**

## Why this order

Not a roadmap — a constraint on which loops can ship before which. Three gradients ascend together: **activation energy** (Loop 1 asks nothing of a visitor; Loop 13 requires a community that's already organized capital and founded businesses), **belief required** (Loop 1 is pre-political; Loop 11 requires believing pooled ownership is real), and **stake accumulated** (every loop produces an event in a Member's record — a follow, a Page made, a post — so by Family 4 a Member's deeper participation is anchored in real history, not abstract willingness).

Each loop leans on the ones above it: pooling works because the community already knows each other through gathering, sharing, and trade. Skip Family 1 and the platform is a directory; skip Family 3 and pooling has no economic substrate. When a scenario advances a deeper-loop behavior without the upstream substrate earned, that's the pattern to push back on. **Beta covers Families 1 and 3 and a member's first step into 4 (a Page they steward); Family 2 waits on messaging.**
