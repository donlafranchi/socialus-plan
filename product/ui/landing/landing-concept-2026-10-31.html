We want decent people to find each other and work together on a better future. That starts with something as small as meeting up.

We don't need another reason to keep scrolling, get lost in a feed, get distracted, and drift apart. We need a way to get out of this house, get to know each other, and do things together. That's why SocialUs was created.

The platform should work for you, not make you work for an algorithm. We are people who love our town and want to make it better. Finding a group, making a plan, meeting someone face to face, that's the point.

Somewhere to go.
People to see.

A neighborhood café. A group that gets together on Tuesday afternoons. A simple group that meets in the park when you can see your neighbor by name.

We want to help bring back 'third spaces'—places beyond home and work where people can naturally gather. SocialUs helps people find each other and organize. The goal is to get us somewhere to grow in the things and people you care about.
