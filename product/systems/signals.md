# Signals (Open and Hidden)

Members wear marks — public or private — to find each other. The same engine powers both kinds.

## What hashtags are and when to use them

Hashtags group things and people around a shared interest, event, or cause. They're how people join a conversation. Use them when you want to find others who care about the same thing, without needing to know their names first.

Examples: #farmersmarket #woodworking #carfree #sacramentobikeros

Don't use them for everything. A post about your daily shop doesn't need a tag. A post about something you care deeply about — and where others who care might find you — does.

**In SocialUs:** hashtags work on Posts (not yet built; see #484). Signals are different — they describe a member or a Page, not a post.

## Signals: the system

One engine. Two kinds.

### Open signals

Explicit, public marks worn on a member's Page or profile. From a curated list chosen by the platform.

**How they work:**
- Pick one or more from the list (e.g., "Woodworker," "Parent," "Farmer," "Vegan," "Dog trainer").
- Tap any signal and see everyone wearing it.
- Help people find their people.

**When to use:** when you're proud of it and want to be found by others who share it.

**Examples:** trades, hobbies, roles, values you live out loud.

### Hidden signals

Private marks for people who want to find their people but could be targeted if they wore a public mark.

**How they work:**
- Unlocked through Help, by asking or seeking.
- Only holders can see who else holds it. Outsiders see nothing.
- Wear it, then find others with it.
- No counts, no membership lists that leak.

**When to use:** identity, faith, health, politics, survivors, people who depend on privacy to stay safe.

**Examples:** parents who've lost a child, people living with chronic illness, LGBTQ+ folks in areas where it isn't safe, people fleeing harm, political beliefs, religious identity.

**The blue dot** is the first hidden signal. It's playful, not heavy — but the same privacy protection applies.

## The help and discovery engine

Members find signals by:
- **Reading help.** "Want a blue dot?" in Page creation help unlocks the blue dot.
- **Asking questions.** Seeker questions in Help ("Are there features I haven't discovered yet?") hint at signals without giving them away.
- **Searching help.** Keyword search over all signal content surfaces signals that match what people ask about.
- **Tapping a signal.** Once unlocked, they appear on a member's Page or profile, and tapping one shows everyone wearing it.

## Post tags vs. signals (relation)

**Tags** (#484, tag review) label a *post*: what the post is about.
**Signals** describe a *person or Page*: who they are, what they care about, who to find.

Both help people discover each other, but at different times and scales.

## One engine for both

Both open and hidden signals use the same mechanism:
1. The signal (name, description, icon or mark).
2. How it's unlocked (always public for open; help/seeking for hidden).
3. "Find others with this" (public search for open; members-only for hidden).
4. Privacy rules that differ by kind (fully public for open; members-only for hidden).

## Tiers

### T1 — Beta

- **Blue dot only.** The first hidden signal, playful and lighthearted.
- Help system: static pages, keyword search, seeker questions (see #534).
- Unlocked from "Want a blue dot?" in Page creation help.
- Members who unlock it see the toggle in Edit Page.

### T2 — After beta

- **Open signals.** Curated list, public marks, anyone can see holders.
- Richer help and discovery.
- Admin tools to manage signal lists.

### T3 — Future

- **Hidden signals.** Full suite of private signals for identity, health, politics, survivors.
- Agent-backed question box to discover signals by asking anything. Answers stay evasive so people figure signals out.
  - Example: **"What's the blue circle in some Page corners?"** — "For people who like blue dots."
- Safety guardrails and moderation tools.
- Counsel approval on data handling.
