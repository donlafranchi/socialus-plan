# Discovery

*Index of every product doc and its settled rules: [`../README.md`](../README.md).*

One scoring core powers the home feed, Explore, search, related-items, and notification ranking — a graph + place + time engine, not a watch-time optimizer. Each engagement (RSVP, pledge, show-up, return) is heavy and meaningful; volume is local, not global, so the system has to rank well in low-data regimes and degrade gracefully for a new Member or a new Location. [for now — the tool side only; newer direction 2026-10-12, see PRINCIPLES.md "two sides"; under review]

## Hard constraints — load-bearing, not tuning knobs

**Ranking doesn't favour a member because of payment, size or follower count, or amplify corporate shells over Members** (2026-09-30). This is the single highest-leverage place a chains-vs-locals bias could enter the platform — once a size-correlated signal is in the score, it's in every surface that uses the score. The constraint lives in the scoring formula itself, not in review process, so "just rank by popularity" is structurally unavailable without modifying the scorer — which is exactly the point where policy review has to happen.

**Personal businesses are first-class; no "verified business" boost.** **Communities are emergent — never auto-assign a Member to a Community-scoped feed.**

## Computed, not stored — the community-awareness feed

The locality-first feed is computed at query time from a Member's private place-interests and interest tags — no per-location "follow" edge table participates in feed generation. This is deliberate: a stored follow-edge set turns every product question toward "how do we get more of them," which is the engagement-metric trap. Computation over the Member's own private interests keeps the feed structurally Member-driven and resistant to follow-graph gaming. A materialized view or cache under the same query is fine as a performance optimization; a stored edge that becomes its own product surface ("12 members follow this venue") is not.

## Inactive businesses are demoted, never hidden or state-changed

A `kind='business'` Group with no qualifying platform activity (posts, fulfillments, gathering occurrences) over a rolling window gets a surfacing-weight demotion in promoted positions — never archived, never a state change, never a public "inactive" label, still fully reachable by direct URL or explicit search. This keeps two questions separate that lifecycle machinery used to conflate: *does the Group continue to exist* (answered by membership alone) versus *does anyone see it in a promoted surface* (answered here, by observable action only — never by the platform guessing whether a business is "really" dormant, which it has no reliable signal for).

## Weights are hand-tuned and in-code on purpose

Every weight change is a code-review event, not a config toggle — a config-driven knob would let ranking drift without an explicit decision, which is exactly the door engagement-optimization would walk through disguised as a routine tuning change. Weights move to data-driven tuning only once real behavioral data exists to tune against.

## Anti-patterns — do not build

Watch-time or dwell-time as an objective. Rows that refill on their own, or that run past an honest stop card: Home's rows keep going, then stop at "You've seen what's new this week", and anything older shows only on a tap (2026-10-04). Any boost tied to payment, "promoted" status, or business size. Auto-membership in a Group from engagement alone — soft signals (follows, attendance) compute at query time and never write a membership row. Unlogged ranking — every call must log its candidate set and score breakdown, because that log is the only path to a smarter ranker later. ## Rulings
- **2026-10-05 — The map's default is a mix: dated posts (upcoming, soonest first), new Pages, recently active Pages and posts, and curated "interesting" Pages. The proportions and a pin cap are currently one tunable setting (`app_settings.map_mix` in `socialus-web`), an even split to start, changed without a deploy. Revisit with beta data by 2026-11-15.** Don: *"map defaults should be a mix. we need to tweak that. capture that somewhere where we can play with it. it should have things with dates as well as other interesting, new, or recent pages/posts. I don't have a good answer without useful data."* Filters narrow within the mix; an empty map offers to widen; signed out, no pins (F093). Which bucket each shown pin came from is counted per day, with no member or session, so the mix can be tuned. `socialus-web` #331. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=explore,map,ranking]
- **2026-09-15 — What counts as interaction: any deliberate act by the author, and any deliberate act by anyone else. Viewing is neither.** Don: *"If the author updates it. Or a user interacts with the page more than viewing."* **Enumerated in `planning/STALENESS.md` rather than left as a principle** — author edits a Page or an entry, author announces, someone responds (interest · rsvp · save · pledge · purchase · support), someone joins, someone follows. **A view and a search impression never count.** **The date is written by the action handler that already commits each write and its event row in the same transaction** — nothing separate watches, no job runs. Half the acts have no writer yet, which is the existing missing-writer list.
- **2026-09-15 — Settled: staleness is 90 days, one number everywhere, not per entry type.** A per-type table is a config surface nobody maintains, and **the seasonal producer is already answered by the design — sorting last is not hiding, and one edit brings it back.**
- **2026-09-15 — Staleness is one general rule: everything carries a last-interaction date, and anything quiet for 90 days sorts last rather than being hidden.** Don: *"We should date these things and not display things that haven't had any interaction in 90 days. Or show them last."* **He offered both; sorting last is taken.** Hiding something without telling its author is how people find out their listing was invisible for a month, and **`model.md` already rules that browse's default is inclusion and anything excluded needs a recorded reason — quiet is a reason to rank below, not to exclude.** The author sees the quiet state on their own Page only; **editing or announcing revives it, with no renew button.** Written up in `planning/STALENESS.md`.
- **2026-09-15 — A view must never count as interaction, and that one is forced rather than chosen.** `surfaces.md` rules that ordering *"may never use what keeps you scrolling"*, and a view is exactly that; counting views would make this an engagement metric through the back door. **What does count is Don's: a response is obviously yes, a follow or join probably, an announcement by the author probably, an edit arguable, a view and a search impression no.** [for now — the tool side only; newer direction 2026-10-12, see PRINCIPLES.md "two sides"; under review]
- **2026-09-15 — Confirmed against the ordering rule rather than assumed: last-interaction sorting is permitted twice over.** `surfaces.md` allows locality, recency, declared-interest boost **and genuine community response** — last interaction is recency, and responses are community response, ratified 2026-09-12.
- **2026-09-15 — This replaces the wonder expiry rather than sitting beside it.** The cut expiry **deleted** a wonder at 90 days; this **demotes** it after 90 quiet days and the author revives it by doing something ordinary. **The removal stands and this is the better version.** Open: whether 90 days is one number everywhere or varies by entry type — a seasonal producer who lists once a year is the case that argues against one number.
- **2026-09-12 — Reverses the earlier same-day entry that said ordering by response count is forbidden.** That entry was itself a correction of a wider error, and it was wrong in its second half. **Both versions were an agent narrowing the rule beyond anything Don stated — not Don changing his mind.** Recorded twice because it was got wrong twice: first as "a response must not be a social signal", then as "displaying a count is fine but ordering by it is forbidden". The rule is money, not response.
- **2026-09-12 — Correction: showing a count and ranking by it are different things, and only the second is forbidden.** The 2026-09-10 entries treated "never an input to ordering" and "not a social signal" as one commitment. They are not. Displaying a response count on an event is information a person uses to decide, and is fine. **Ordering browse results by it** is what the no-sold-visibility and no-engagement-ranking commitments forbid. The blanket version was an agent's conflation, not a ruling.
