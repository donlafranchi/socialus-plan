# Easter eggs and help

Hidden features found only by people who ask and seek, so like-minded members find each other.

## Purpose

Easter eggs reward curiosity. Members who discover them are the ones most likely to find peers who care about the same things. The blue dot is egg #1.

## Help system

Help pages organized by area:
- Page creation
- Posting
- Events
- Explore and finding
- Account and sign-in
- Reporting and safety
- Members and how SocialUs is different

Each area has an FAQ and searchable help articles. Eggs are unlocked through help content — by reading, asking, or searching the right questions.

## Starter FAQ and seeker questions

### Page creation

**Real questions (California Vibe voice)**
- How do I make a Page? — Easy. Tap Create, give it a name and location, pick what kind it is, and you're done.
- Can I have more than one Page? — Yep. One for the shop, one for the farmers market booth, whatever.
- How do I change what kind of Page it is (Business, Group, Organization)? — It's in Edit Page. Change it anytime.
- Why would I make a Page instead of just posting? — A Page is your thing; it stays. Posts are announcements about what's happening.
- Can I delete a Page? — You can archive it (it hides for now) or delete it (it's gone for good).
- **"Want a blue dot?"** — See something special you care about? Find out here. [UNLOCKS: blue dot egg]

**Seeker questions**
- "Are there secret things I can unlock?" — Maybe. Try asking around.
- "How do I make my Page stand out?" — Be real. The rest follows.

### Posting

**Real questions**
- What can I post about? — Something for sale, something you're teaching, a gathering, a question, anything that helps people find you and find each other.
- How many photos can I add to a post? — One per post for now. Make it count.
- Can I edit or delete a post after it's up? — Edit anytime. Delete's gone for good.
- Who sees my posts? — Whoever's looking in your area. If your Page is private, just the people you let in.
- What's the difference between a post and a Page? — A Page is you (or your shop); a post is what's happening right now.

**Seeker questions**
- "What kinds of posts do people make here?" — Look around. You'll see.
- "Is there more to posting than just pictures?" — Stick around.

### Events

**Real questions**
- How do I let people know about a gathering? — Post about it. Include when and where and what it's for.
- Can people RSVP? — They can see it and say hi. Formal RSVPs come later.
- What info goes in an event post? — Time, place, what to bring or do, who it's for. Short and clear.
- Who can see that I'm going to something? — Just you and them. Not broadcast.

**Seeker questions**
- "Is there a way to organize bigger gatherings?" — Asking good questions helps.

### Explore and finding

**Real questions**
- How does the map work? — It shows what's around you. Tap a pin to see what it is.
- Can I search for something specific? — Filter by what you're looking for. Or browse.
- What if there's nothing here yet? — Be the first. Or help someone else out.
- Why does the map show some things but not others? — Private Pages stay private. Public ones show up.
- Can I filter what I see? — Yep. Find what matters to you.

**Seeker questions**
- "Is there a way to find people who care about what I care about?" — Look closer.
- "What's really going on in the map view?" — The answer's in there somewhere.

### Account and sign-in

**Real questions**
- How do I change my password? — Account settings. It's straightforward.
- Can I sign in with email or another way? — Email link is the way in. No password juggling.
- What's my zip code for? — Keeps everything local. It sets what you see.
- Can I delete my account? — Yep. That's final.
- Where's my privacy info? — In the footer. Real talk about what we do with your data.

### Reporting and safety

**Real questions**
- How do I report something? — Tap Report on any post or Page. It's private.
- What happens after I report something? — Someone looks at it. They decide what happens next.
- Can I undo a report? — Once it's in, it's in. Be sure.
- Who reads my report? — Real people here who care about keeping things safe.

### Members and how SocialUs is different

**Real questions**
- Who owns SocialUs? — Members do. No VC, no ads that sell your data.
- Does this app make money from ads? — Nope. We take a tiny cut on what people trade.
- Why is it free to make a Page? — It should be. You're the product we care about, not the ad.
- Can I use this for my business? — That's the whole idea.
- Is this like Facebook or Instagram? — It looks different because it is. No feed, no algorithm. Just you and what's around.

## Rules for eggs

- Never shown in the main UI.
- Unlocked by asking, seeking, or reading help.
- Light and playful — "a wink and a nod."
- Never pay-to-unlock.
- Never extractive (don't ask for data or permissions to unlock).
- Discoverable by the people who'd use them most.

## Tiers

### T1 — Beta

- Static help pages per area, with simple keyword search.
- **Blue dot (#525):** toggle is NOT visible in Edit Page. It's unlocked from the help article ("Want a blue dot?"). Once unlocked, the toggle shows for that member on all their Pages.
- Unlocked status stored per member.
- Depends on: help pages for at least Page creation and posting.

### T2

- More eggs, each with its own discover path.
- Richer help: images, short videos, member stories.
- Admin tools to see which eggs are most discovered.

### T3

- Agent-backed question box: members ask anything, the agent searches help content and reveals eggs when the question suggests they'd use them.
- Requires: Anthropic API key with a cost cap set by the PM.
