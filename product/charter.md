# Charter

**A work in progress. Guidelines until the organization is formed.** Rebuilt by `regen-charter` from the charter-tagged rulings; change a ruling there, not this page.

## Mission

**SocialUs: the people's platform. Members first, always.**

SocialUs exists to serve its members, not shareholders, and to give them a platform to build a better future.

## Three flips

We are trying to flip how technology gets used.

**1. Bring people together in person, not stand in for it.** Technology here points people at each other and at a place and a time. It does not replace meeting.
- We currently order what you see by where it is and how recent it is, never by what holds your attention or what someone paid for. [for now — Find only; newer direction 2026-10-12, see PRINCIPLES.md "two sides"; under review]
- Ranking is a place, time and relationship engine, not a watch-time optimizer. Browse is complete, not ranked by your interests. [for now — Find only; newer direction 2026-10-12, see PRINCIPLES.md "two sides"; under review]
- We build for meeting in person, and we measure whether neighbours actually met.

**2. Spread wealth among the many, not concentrate it among the few.** What people earn here stays with the people who earned it and the places they live.
- We are never extractive: we take no value from people without giving something back.
- We currently take a share of transaction income on member commerce at a rate the income plan sets, and our fees favour no member over another.
- No member can pay to rank above another: visibility is not sold by default. [for now — Find only; paid promotion on Discover is open for design after beta (Don, 2026-10-12); under review]
- Everyone who makes or hosts something here starts on equal footing: a Page costs nothing to create.
- We collect data to benefit our members, never to enrich shareholders or investors, and we make it easy to opt out.

**3. Design for kindness; make cruelty hard.** We help each other be good to one another.
- A first report gives the poster a courtesy notice and a chance to fix it. A second report hides the post and sends it to a person from our team. Threats of harm and sensitive content hide at once. Notices are kind, in the app, never by email.
- Reporting is for real problems: misused reports carry a visible consequence for the reporter.
- We currently default to what people would expect socially about who sees what.

## Real examples

Third-party summaries; none is a promise for us. They show it can be done.

- **Front Porch Forum** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_Porch_Forum)): local forums with human moderators; staying local and keeping a person in the loop makes respect the norm.
- **Nextdoor's Kindness Reminder** ([Nextdoor, 2019](https://blog.nextdoor.com/2019/09/18/announcing-our-new-feature-to-promote-kindness-in-neighborhoods)): a prompt before an unkind reply; in the early test negative comments fell by 20%. A nudge beats a ban.
- **Instagram's pre-post warning** ([NPR via MPR, 2019](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2019/07/10/npr-instagram-to-flag-hateful-comments-before-you-send-them)): a chance to reflect, and no one removed.
- **REI** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/REI)): member-owned, with a yearly dividend on what members spent. Wealth can flow back in proportion to taking part.
- **Stocksy United** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stocksy_United)): a cooperative whose artists co-own it, one vote each. The people who make the content can share the profit.

## Helping members not feel squeezed

For children born in the 1980s only about half earn more than their parents, down from about 90% for those born in 1940 ([Chetty and others, Science 2017](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22910)). The product can help in five ways:

1. **Earning.** Put a member's own skill in front of neighbours for free: Pages for makers and services, no pay-to-rank. [for now — Find only; paid promotion on Discover is open for design after beta (Don, 2026-10-12); under review]
2. **Lower costs.** Make what is free, cheap or shared nearby easy to find: swaps, borrowing, volunteering, group buys.
3. **Skills.** Classes, workshops and series from neighbours, and Pages for "teach what you know".
4. **Connections.** Meet in person the people who lead to work, tools and mentors; "I'm coming" makes a gathering real.
5. **Prove it.** We measure our success by how much we improve our members' lives, financially and socially.
