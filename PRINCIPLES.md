# PRINCIPLES.md

**The soul, the defaults, and the principles under them: the why behind the Charter and every ruling.** The soul is what we believe; every principle and ruling answers to it, and where one conflicts with it, the soul wins and the other is reworded or retired. The defaults decide anything the rules don't cover. Few, and clearer over time. When a ruling tests a principle, the principle is reworded in place, its date becomes the day it was sharpened, and the old wording goes to git. Dated rulings live inline under `## Rulings` in the scenario they govern, moving to the spine or system doc when it ships; their words are not edited. A principle never carries history, examples or a list of the rulings it produced, and `scripts/markers.py principles` fails anything else here. Where each kind of thing goes: `ops-pattern/process/WHERE-THINGS-GO.md`.

## Soul

SocialUs: what we believe.

- <a id="decent"></a>**2026-10-25 — We assume the best of each other. [always]** People are decent, so we design for decency and keep simple ways to handle the times they aren't. "Thank you for being decent to each other." [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,ethos build=none]
- <a id="benefit-all"></a>**2026-10-25 — Everything we do benefits all of us, not a few. [always]** We'll use the tools other apps use (data, algorithms, AI) because the problem was never the tools; it's a small group getting rich from them. Here the benefit flows back to members. AI helps people; it doesn't replace them. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,ethos build=none]
- <a id="you-choose"></a>**2026-10-25 — You choose. [always]** We give people options and let them decide, and we push choices down as close to each person as we can. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,ethos build=none]
- <a id="out-of-the-way"></a>**2026-10-25 — We build the system, then get out of the way. [always]** People organize, discover and help each other; we make that easy, then step back. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,ethos build=none]
- <a id="works-for-you"></a>**2026-10-25 — It works for you. [always]** Local discovery and organizing that comes to you, without chasing followers or feeding a platform. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,ethos build=none]
- <a id="locals-supporting-locals"></a>**2026-10-30 — Locals supporting locals. ## Principles

Each one is how a line of the soul applies here.

- <a id="pitch"></a>**2026-10-25 — SocialUs works for the member; the member does not work for it.** `changed: 2026-10-25` Algorithms rank for the member, not the platform. `cap-1` timely announcements · `cap-2` discovery without searching · `cap-3` their own signals · `cap-4` control over what shows · `cap-5` current without following many · `cap-6` owners served · `cap-7` holds up against Yelp, Google and social apps · `cap-8` honest origin, no badge for everything. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,explore,announcements,you,pages build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — The app has two sides: Find (the tool), where a person looks for something specific, and Discover (the fun), where a person explores with nothing in mind and an algorithm may serve what interests them. [always]** This is the newer direction: where an earlier ruling, principle or Charter line conflicts with it, that line is reviewed and updated. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,explore,discovery build=none]
- **2026-10-25 — Data serves the members it comes from. [always]** SocialUs may collect what it can use, including device and coarse location signals, to make the app better for members; no one else profits from it, it is never sold, and opting out is one easy step. Between members, sharing is opt-in, granular, visible and revocable, and a member is private by default. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,privacy,data build=none]
- **2026-10-25 — In public, locality is coarse; precision stays private.** Suggestions and feeds may use roughly where a member is, for their benefit; no other member learns a member's precise location. Whether distance is shown is decided for this version only, in `product/systems/places.md`. The one exception is a Page address its owner chooses to make public. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,map,privacy build=none]
- **2026-10-25 — Promotion earns its place by serving members. [always]** On Discover a paid or promoted placement is allowed when it is marked as promoted, a member would want to see it, its surplus flows back to members, and a member can turn promotion off. Find is never reordered by payment. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,explore,discovery build=none]
- **2026-10-25 — AI helps people and doesn't stand in for them; what an assistant knows belongs to the member. [always]** Context is exportable and deletable; it may improve SocialUs's own member-serving models unless the member opts out, and it is never sold or used to train anyone else's. The assistant never holds the keys: credentials are minted per turn at the network edge. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,ai build=none]
- **2026-10-25 — What never-extractive rules out: selling member data, a revenue line that works better when a member is stuck, and venture capital (no priced rounds, no plan that only works at venture scale). [always]** [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,fees build=none]
- **2026-10-16 — Local means money comes back to where it was generated; it is never a distance, a map or a metro. [always]** A boundary the platform draws (an MSA, a ring, a radius) can place a Page on a map but never makes it local. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,badges,pages build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — The platform is the technology layer, never the bank.** A chartered partner holds member funds; the platform holds none and stores no card numbers. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,fees build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — People, not shells: one word for a person, member; every role is derived, never stored; and a business is the people doing its work.** No account types, no business entity, no corporate shells, no ownership transfer, no succession. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,members,pages build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — Groups are joined, never assigned.** No geofenced or auto-populated membership; suggesting a Group is fine, joining is the member's act. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,groups build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — The platform records facts; it never performs legal acts or speaks legal language.** [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,legal build=none]
- **2026-10-25 — Measure what happens inside the app; the north star is time and money together.** A number tunes the app for members and is never raised for its own sake. No claimed economic-impact multipliers. Demand is measured before it is built, including our own: "Other" text, not-built-yet taps and idea-floats are counted, never auto-promoted to a commitment. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,metrics build=none]
