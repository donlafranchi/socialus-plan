# PRINCIPLES.md

**The principles: the why behind the Charter and every ruling.** Few, and clearer over time. When a ruling tests a principle, the principle is reworded in place, its date becomes the day it was sharpened, and the old wording goes to git. Dated rulings live inline under `## Rulings` in the scenario they govern, moving to the spine or system doc when it ships; their words are not edited. A principle never carries history, examples or a list of the rulings it produced, and `scripts/markers.py principles` fails anything else here. Where each kind of thing goes: `ops-pattern/process/WHERE-THINGS-GO.md`.

## Principles
- **2026-10-12 — The app has two sides: Find (the tool), where a person looks for something specific, and Discover (the fun), where a person explores with nothing in mind and an algorithm may serve what interests them. [always]** This is the newer direction: where an earlier ruling, principle or Charter line conflicts with it, that line is reviewed and updated. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,explore,discovery build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — Everything the platform touches serves the people it touches.** A feature whose value to the platform costs a member is out, however well built. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — Wealth circulates; it is not extracted.** No selling member data, no revenue line that works better when a member is stuck, no venture capital: no priced rounds, and no plan that only works at venture scale. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,fees build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — The platform is the technology layer, never the bank.** A chartered partner holds member funds; the platform holds none and stores no card numbers. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,fees build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — Paid visibility passes a member-benefit gate; it isn't banned by fiat.** "It funds the platform" is the product half of the argument and fails alone. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,explore build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — People, not shells: one word for a person, member; every role is derived, never stored; and a business is the people doing its work.** No account types, no business entity, no corporate shells, no ownership transfer, no succession. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,members,pages build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — Groups are joined, never assigned.** No geofenced or auto-populated membership. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,groups build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — Sharing is opt-in, granular, visible and revocable, and a member is private by default.** No pre-checked boxes and no default-on sharing. A member's discoverability defaults to private; what they make (Items, Groups) surfaces on its own settings, and a business Page stays public for commerce while the member behind it stays separately gated. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,privacy build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — In public, locality is coarse; precision stays private.** No location-scoped feeds or messaging. Whether distance is shown is decided for this version only, in `product/systems/places.md`. The one exception is a Page address its owner chooses to make public (the 2026-09-09 ruling). [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,map,privacy build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — The platform records facts; it never performs legal acts or speaks legal language.** [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,legal build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — What an assistant knows belongs to the member; the assistant never holds the keys.** Context is exportable, deletable and never trained on; credentials are minted per turn at the network edge. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,ai build=none]
- **2026-10-12 — Measure what happens inside the app; the north star is time and money together.** No claimed economic-impact multipliers. Demand is measured before it is built, including our own: "Other" text, not-built-yet taps and idea-floats are counted, never auto-promoted to a commitment. [binds tiers=planning,code surfaces=principles,metrics build=none]
