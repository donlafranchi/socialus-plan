## Story

*The PM, 2026-11-01, verbatim:* "We want to limit so we stay solvent financially. right now we have no way to earn money. If we find that users like what we're building then that's the immediate next thing we build. we can't afford to open to the masses until we have a way to pay for it since we're not taking VC money."

Priya in Davis gives $8 a month. Davis's page shows the month: what came in, what covers servers and support, and what is still short. When Davis's cost is covered and it has its 100 makers and 1,000 supporters, it opens.

## Acceptance

1. **Contribute:** a member gives $1 to $25 a month, $8 suggested, annual at 10x, as already specced in `product/systems/contributions.md`. Nothing is gated behind any amount.
2. **Cost coverage per market:** each market page shows money in against the first milestone (lights on), as counts and dollars, never names.
3. **Opening needs it:** a market opens only when its goals (F118) and its cost coverage are both met (PRINCIPLES.md, 2026-11-01).
4. **Sign-up and waiting lists stay open and free.**
5. Copy and wording follow `contributions.md` § Copy rules; every new string goes to the PM.

## Not this

Selling member data, venture money, a fee that works better when a member is stuck, any payout (counsel first), paid placement.

[open-question owner=don raised=2026-11-01] Which comes first: member contributions (A, recommended, already specced), transaction income through Pages (B), or marked promotion on Discover (C)? The payment rail is open in `contributions.md`.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** Partly: Patreon and Open Collective take money but are not tied to a community's opening.
- **Only locals know?** Yes: only locals decide a community is worth paying for.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: a shared goal people fund together.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: the figure moves with each payment.
- **Who keeps it current?** Nobody; it is computed from payments.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: more members cover more cost.

Soul: `#decent`, `#you-choose`. **Path: new territory** (money). Precedents: Wikipedia's recurring-donor model, Patreon, Kickstarter's goal bar.
