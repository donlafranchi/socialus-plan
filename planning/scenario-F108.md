## Story

Dana writes a sharp reply to a neighbour's post. Before it goes up, the app says, kindly, that this may hurt and asks if she wants to change it. She rewrites one line and posts. Another time she posts it as written; nothing stops her. Nobody was told, and no score on Dana changed.

## Acceptance

Not written. Brought to the PM first (see the options below); criteria follow his call.

## Not this

Blocking a post. A score or label on a person. Telling anyone but the author. Email.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** No: Nextdoor and Instagram each have a version. Ours ties to the Charter's kindness line.
- **Only locals know?** No: the pause is generic; the cutoff comes from our own log.
- **Gets people together?** Indirectly: fewer neighbours leave after a bad exchange.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes, once the cutoff is read from the moderation log.
- **Who keeps it current?** The operator, from the log (AI-scoring ruling, 2026-10-11).
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: more log data gives a better cutoff.

**Path: new territory, for the PM later.** It reads a member's draft before it is posted, which touches privacy and member trust, and it was left out of the Charter until ruled (2026-10-11).

Precedents: [Nextdoor Kindness Reminder, 2019](https://blog.nextdoor.com/2019/09/18/announcing-our-new-feature-to-promote-kindness-in-neighborhoods) (in the early test negative comments fell by 20%); [Instagram's pre-post warning, 2019](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2019/07/10/npr-instagram-to-flag-hateful-comments-before-you-send-them). Both are a pause, not a block.

**For the PM (A/B/C):**
- A) **Pause with a scored draft** (recommended): the AI scores the draft; above a cutoff set from our log, the author sees a kind pause once and may post anyway. The draft is scored, not stored.
- B) **Pause only after a report history**: only authors with upheld reports see it, no draft scoring. Weaker, and starts a label on a person.
- C) **No pause at launch**: revisit after the shadow data (two weeks, 2026-10-11 ruling).

Recommend A once shadow data exists, which means C until then.
