## Your two questions, answered

- **"Three rings means an hour of Sacramento, correct?" Nearly.** The rings (hood, town, area) centre on each member's home, not on a market's centre; the market is the outer ring. Its edge is your 10-27 hour, with two exceptions: a town nearer another hub goes there (Lodi 49 minutes, Stockton 57, Fairfield 53), and Grass Valley and Nevada City (76 and 84) came in because they are too small alone. Minutes are free-flow; Friday traffic is worse.
- **"How far are people driving to a market?" About 10 miles, as best anyone has measured.** No Sacramento survey I could read gives it. In US metros over a million people, farmers' market customers come 10.4 miles and **vendors 46.8** (USDA, 2006). Shopping trips average 5.8 miles and social and recreation trips 17.1 (2022 national survey). An hour is destination reach: an event, Apple Hill, a Saturday. It isn't weekly shopping.

## What each one gets wrong

- **A. Four counties.** Nothing to build. But 68 seeded Pages sit in Solano, Nevada, Sutter and Yuba (2026-11-06 count): Explore shows them and "is my town open?" would say no to the people next door.
- **B. Rings to a population.** It stops at the wrong places: ring 1 leaves out Davis, and ring 2 brings in Stockton, Lodi and Antioch before Placerville, Yuba City or Vacaville, because one big neighbour swallows the count. Adjacency is right for ordering inside a market (F104), not for its edge.
- **C. Population centres.** Your idea, and it reads well: it adds Yuba City–Marysville and western Nevada County. It also pulls in Colusa, most of Amador and Gridley, and drops Vacaville and Dixon (13% of Solano's out-commuters come this way). **The 10-27 method already uses your 50,000**: a town that size, over an hour from any other hub, becomes a hub of its own.
- **D. Commuting zone.** Whole counties: it puts Placerville and El Dorado Hills in **Reno** and brings Truckee and the Tahoe shore in, both against F103. The Census metro (2.40M, El Dorado whole) and combined area (2.68M, with Truckee) split the same towns differently.
- **E. The hour (recommended).** It keeps your ruling and matches how people come: about 81% of the 125,098 people who commute into Sacramento County start in a county E covers; A's counties are 71%. It's approved and ticketed. Its cost: thinner cover at the fringe in the first weeks.

## Shoppers from further out than businesses

**It works, if the line is drawn on the Page, not the person.** Nobody has an account type here (2026-09-14), so two kinds of sign-up by zip would mean asking people which they are. Two lists do it with nothing to ask:

1. **Page list: the market's edge (E).** A Page shows in Sacramento if its own place is inside (F104.4). A Lodi grower who sells at a Sacramento market is in, by that market; the Page says where it usually shows up (2026-09-30).
2. **People list: the edge plus towns within 75 minutes.** Stockton, Lodi, Fairfield, Vallejo, Napa, Jackson and more: about 48 more towns, 48 zips, 0.98M people. Someone there is asked once: wait for your own town, or join Sacramento now? They count where they choose (approved 10-27, § 4).
3. **Any other zip: the waitlist**, as today.

**Recommended: hold it for the beta's second month, or 100 makers, whichever is first.** It needs a sign-up screen with your words, and it isn't on your January 6 must-have line. Supporters are the easy side; a wide crowd arriving to few makers is what "creators are the real gate" warns about.

**The evidence cuts one way you may not expect:** at farmers' markets it's the sellers who come from far and the shoppers who are near. So "businesses must be inside" should mean where they sell, never where they live.

## Precedents

- **Seller limited, shopper not:** [GrowNYC Greenmarket](https://grownyc.org/greenmarket) takes producers from 120 miles south, 170 east and west, 250 north [secondary: search summary]; California certified markets take California producers; shoppers come from anywhere.
- **Coverage drawn from the store:** [DoorDash](https://help.doordash.com/en-au/merchants/article/what-is-a-delivery-radius-or-a-delivery-area-on-doordash) sets an area around each store, and any customer inside it may order [search summary].
- **Home locked, reach small:** [Nextdoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nextdoor) ties a member to a verified address; a business's free posts reach about two miles [secondary, 2019–2024].
- **A region you choose:** [Craigslist](https://www.craigslist.org/about/help/posting/create) asks the poster to choose a location, and anyone may read any region.
- **Conflict:** Nextdoor locks people and Craigslist doesn't. People follow Craigslist here and Pages follow Greenmarket. Uber's driver and rider areas: not verified, not relied on.

## Five Deadly Sins, plainly

- **Unrealistic schedule:** E is a script, a reviewed diff and about six rows. Pick by December 4 and it lands before the dry run.
- **Scope creep:** the two-list sign-up stays off the January 6 line.
- **Gold plating:** B and C need a new rule engine to move a handful of towns.
- **Vague requirements:** "an hour" is now a town list, below.
- **Unclear acceptance:** one zip tested on each side of every edge county.

Workers commuting into Sacramento County, 2016–2020 ([EDD, from Census ACS](https://labormarketinfo.edd.ca.gov/file/commute-maps/sacramento2020.pdf), read from the map 2026-11-23): Placer 44,869; Yolo 23,221; El Dorado 21,015; San Joaquin 9,847; Solano 5,384; Yuba 3,009; Sutter 2,489; Amador 1,864; Nevada 1,151; total in 125,098. Another 563,061 live and work in the county.

Share of each county's out-commuters headed to Sacramento, Placer, Yolo or El Dorado (EDD): Nevada 69%, Yuba 44% (Sutter takes 42%), Sutter 36% (Yuba takes 36%), Yuba and Sutter together, leaving out each other, 66%; Amador 59%; Colusa 51%; **Solano 13%; San Joaquin 12%** (38% of San Joaquin's go to Alameda County). These are whole counties: Vacaville alone was not separated from Vallejo.

Trips, national: [NHTS 2022, table 3-3](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/73764): shopping 5.8 miles (2017: 7.1), errands 8.6, social and recreation 17.1 (margin 2.7; 2017: 10.4). 2017 report: a shopping trip takes 17.5 minutes.

Farmers' markets: USDA 2011, metros over 1 million, customers 10.4 miles and vendors 46.8, from a 2006 survey in which **managers estimated** the distances; read in [Oppenheim 2018](https://sites.geography.unt.edu/~rice/OPPENHEIM-THESIS-2018.pdf), table 2.4, because the USDA file refused the fetch. [Iowa shoppers: 9 miles in 2009](https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/article/2010/1-6/farmersmarketsurvey.html).

Everyday purchases: [Access Development](https://blog.accessdevelopment.com/research-how-far-will-consumers-travel-to-make-routine-purchases): 93% travel 20 minutes or less, but 70% of rural people travel more; a discount company's survey, not a probability sample.

Events: 49% of Golden 1 Center visitors in 2023 came from over 40 miles (Downtown Sacramento Partnership, Placer.ai). **Seen only in a search summary; the report was not opened.** It is an arena, not shopping.

**Not found:** SACOG's 2018 household survey trip lengths (its site refused the fetch; SACOG shares tables on request); visitor origins for Midtown, Davis, the State Fair, Farm-to-Fork or Apple Hill.

<details><summary>How the lists were made, and what's assumed</summary>

- People and boundaries: 2020 Census by county subdivision (CCD), from the Census TIGERweb service. A town is a Census Place (city or CDP), placed by its centre point. Zips are ZCTAs placed by centre point, so counts are within a few of what the built zip lookup will give.
- Minutes: the public OSRM server, free flow, from downtown Sacramento to the largest place in each subdivision. Hubs compared: Stockton, Modesto, Chico, Oakland, Santa Rosa, Reno, Tahoe City.
- B uses touching CCDs (they tile the land; Places don't). `places.md` orders by Places with county remainders, which would give more, smaller rings.
- C joins a smaller town to the 50,000+ centre that is the shortest drive (a stand-in for "most connected"; town-level commuting files were not loaded), then joins a centre to Sacramento by county commuting. Centres in: Sacramento, Elk Grove, Roseville, Arden-Arcade, Citrus Heights, Folsom, Carmichael, Rancho Cordova, Rocklin, Yuba City, Davis, Woodland, West Sacramento, Florin, El Dorado Hills. Lincoln misses at 49,757. C keeps Clarksburg but loses the Delta (Isleton, Walnut Grove, Courtland) and Winters. Read literally, with no joining step, C is 15 separate markets.
- D: the ERS 2020 zone as read on 2026-10-26 (Sacramento, Yolo, Placer, Nevada, Sutter, Yuba, Colusa).
- E applies the approved method: Fairfield is a tie (53 to Sacramento, 54 to Oakland) and the commuting zone breaks it to the Bay Area; the leftover corners of Sutter and Yuba merge in, being too small alone; Truckee stays Lake Tahoe's. Every Placer and El Dorado subdivision but the two Tahoe ones stays, by the 10-11 ruling, though Georgetown and Pollock Pines are 72 minutes.
- The 11-22 ruling says the launch market is "as it stands in the data (the 2026-10-27 drive-time reach)". The data on 2026-11-23 is still A: web #1101 is open and no later migration touches the market's members. Your pick replaces those words.
