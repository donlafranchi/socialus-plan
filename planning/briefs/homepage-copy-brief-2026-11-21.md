**As of 2026-11-21** · Source: the PM's words, reconstructed · Maintained by: hand

## The front door

A chat/search-first front door, like Google or Claude: one box, a few suggestions, a short warm intro.

## What SocialUs is

- **Locals finding and supporting locals.**
- **Two halves.** Discovery is the way in. Cooperation, collaboration and community development is where it's headed. Bring it to life.
- **Search powers the community.** Makers see totals, never who searched.
- **Find what matters to you,** kept broad: no surveys, no politics, no badges.

## How we're different (as intentions)

- It works for you.
- It benefits all of us: wealth circulates and isn't extracted; take what we need and give back.
- We grow only as fast as we can pay for ourselves.
- Data serves you and is never sold; you can see it, change it and delete it.
- No one can stalk anyone.
- You choose.
- We build the system, then get out of the way.
- We assume the best of each other.

## The line to walk

- **Intentions, not promises.**
- Everything except one thing can change if we find a better way.
- The one firm statement: wealth is never extracted (that includes never selling data).
- Avoid claims of guaranteed, forever, numbers, dates, ownership, payout or investors.

## Tone

Warm, plain, neighborly, a little hopeful. Not preachy.

## Already decided

- The line **"Made for us."**
- The terracotta look.
- The two-layer safety line.
