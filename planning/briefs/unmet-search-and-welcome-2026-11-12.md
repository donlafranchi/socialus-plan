# When a search finds nothing, and welcoming new kinds of business

**As of 2026-11-12** · Source: the PM's words below; the shared list (web #1180), the seeder's log of every Page added, F076, F089, F118, F122, the privacy principle (plan #329) · Maintained by: hand (brief; deleted at 30 days or once ruled and placed)

*The PM, 2026-11-12:* "I dont need anymore pages or businesses yet. we do need to refine or categorize what we have. I also want to have a response for when someone searches for something new that we don't have. I was thinking I want to find a skateboard shop and if I search for skateboards and nothing comes up what would that look like. i want an approach to give people the permission to go find one and recommend that they sign up. i want to reward people for getting others to sign up and make it a ceremony where we welcome new kinds of businesses."

*The PM, 2026-11-12, later:* "also I wouldn't mind giving many of these pages default icons instead of photos to allow users to know which pages have owners. And lastly we should probably also come up with different pin styles for the page types. one for businesses, others for groups and others for anything else."

**Bottom line: tidy the list we have now (agents do it, no new Pages); after the waitlist, turn "nothing found" into "bring one here", and welcome the first of each kind with a moment the whole market sees. Your calls: what the reward is, who sees the welcome, and the words.**

## The loop

Search "skateboards" → nothing → "Know a good one? Bring it here" → you add the shop (an unclaimed Page, F118) and send them the claim link → the owner claims → Sacramento sees "Welcome, our first skate shop", and everyone who asked to be reminded is told → you're credited, if you opted in.

Most of the parts exist: search already logs what found nothing (counts only); reminders on a search exist; F118 (approved, next) already has "add a maker you love" and the invite log. New: the screen, the welcome, the reward.

## 2. A search that finds nothing (well-worn shape; words are yours; built after the waitlist)

**Precedents:** [Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/no-results-page): a no-results page is a dead end on about half of sites; give ways forward, not tips alone. [Google Maps](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6320846): "Add a missing place" right where the search came up empty. Thumbtack: no pro listed, so describe the job and pros come to you. [Nextdoor](https://nextcity.org/urbanist-news/building-the-facebook-of-neighborhoods): no neighbourhood yet, so a neighbour founds one.

**The screen, "skateboards" in Sacramento** (draft words):

- **Headline:** Nothing for "skateboards" in Sacramento yet. (call 3)
- **Permission line:** Know a good one? Go say hi, and tell them people here are looking.
- **Add a place you love** (main button): F118's form. Name and website are enough. We match existing Pages first, then make an unclaimed one, then give you a ready-to-send note: "Claim your free Page on SocialUs. People in Sacramento are looking for you."
- **Remind me when one joins:** a reminder on this search (built). Private to the searcher.
- **Try a nearby word**, like Fitness & outdoors or Shops, then **Look at another area** (built).
- **"You're not the only one looking"** only once ten people have searched it in that market (F089's floor). Never a number, never who.

**Logging:** already built. Each search that settles stores the words, the market and how many results came back, never who; deduped by a hash that expires after 10 minutes; off for anyone who turns it off on You. That fits the privacy principle: no one, staff included, can see who searched. Added: the staff dashboard's "found nothing" list becomes **Wanted here**, the top searches with no results per market, the list scouts and the seeder start from once Pages resume.

## 3. Rewards and the welcome (new territory: your calls)

**What's already ruled.** F076 (2026-09-14) says not to build a leaderboard or a referral mechanic. F118 (2026-10-31, newer) already logs every invite and gives recognition only: no cash, credit or perks. It also adds Founders per market and a Movers & Shakers board to come. Your words today ask to "reward people for getting others to sign up". Newer wins, so F076's line goes once you confirm. The open question is whether the reward goes past recognition.

**Precedents:** Yelp Elite: status, events, no pay. Nextdoor's [founding members](https://nextcity.org/urbanist-news/building-the-facebook-of-neighborhoods): set up the neighbourhood and become its leads. Waze and OpenStreetMap: every map edit carries its editor's name, and Waze gives trusted editors an area to look after. Product Hunt: the hunter's name stays on the launch. [Dropbox](https://help.dropbox.com/storage-space/earn-space-referring-friends): space for both sides, capped, self-referrals voided. Airbnb ended its guest referrals. [Co-op patronage](https://archive.grocer.coop/node/7597): surplus returned in proportion to what each member did.

**The welcome** (draft, after call 2): it fires when an **owner claims** the first Page of a finer label in a market, or the first after we add a label for a new kind like skate shops. A seeded unclaimed Page never fires it, so the welcome is always real. The market sees "Welcome, Sacramento's first skate shop" with the Page, for a week. The Page keeps a lasting "First in Sacramento" mark. People who asked to be reminded are told, and the person who brought it in is named only if they opted in.

**Anti-gaming, whatever you pick:** one real mobile per account, checked at sign-up (F081). Credit only when an owner claims, never for adding a Page. First touch is kept for good and each person counts once (F118). Test accounts never count. A board shows names only for people who opted in.

## 5. Map pins by type (look-and-feel: yours)

**Precedents:** Google Maps and Apple Maps tell kinds apart by a glyph in the pin with a colour per family (food, shopping, outdoors). Yelp uses one red pin and puts the difference in the card. [Mapbox's Maki](https://labs.mapbox.com/maki-icons/) is the open icon set built for map pins. Colour alone fails people who can't tell red from green, so shape carries the type and colour supports it.

**The three types** (Pages are already business or group in the database):

- **Business**, a Page that's a business, from farms and bakeries to repair shops: a teardrop pin with its label's glyph.
- **Group**, a Page that's a group, such as a service club, land trust, choir or birding club: a round pin with its label's glyph.
- **Everything else**, a member's own post (lost and found, free, ask): a small dot.
- **Not yet owned:** the same pin, outlined instead of filled, which matches § 4. Claiming fills it.

**Before building:** some seeded nonprofits may be stored as businesses. The pins PR re-checks every unclaimed Page's type against its label from § 1. You approve the look at a live link behind the builders-only flag, then it ships. Filed as socialus-web #1308 (deferred, after the waitlist).

## Your calls, most-unblocking first

1. **The reward for bringing people in.** F076 (2026-09-14) says no referral mechanic; your words today ask for a reward. Newer wins once you confirm.
   - **A (recommended): recognition only,** as F118 ruled on 2026-10-31. A Founder badge per market, "Brought in by" on the welcome, and a board for those who opt in. Costs nothing, can't be bought, and needs no counsel.
   - **B: A, plus a founding-neighbor credit** recorded in the invite log. A later member-benefit program could honour it, co-op style, with no promise now. It needs socialus-legal before any wording, because "a share of future benefit" can read as an investment.
   - **C: cash or account credit per claimed Page,** like Dropbox. It needs the company and counsel first, and it draws people who chase claims instead of real stories.
