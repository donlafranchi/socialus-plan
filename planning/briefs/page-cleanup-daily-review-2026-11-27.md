- Pace: **A, 5 Pages a day** (about 3 months for the 429 live).
- "If this app turned into 'What's going on (nearby and in the next week)?' How do we present this to them?" Chose **A**: finding each business's next dated thing is part of the cleanup.

## The shape, as proposed to the PM

1. **Each day an agent picks 5 existing unclaimed Pages,** worst first (the 291 with stock covers lead).
2. **From the business's own site only** (rule #353), it drafts an improved version: its own photos in place of stock (or "none usable"), a sharper description, hours, address, a contact email for the PM's outreach, links checked, a specific kind ("independent skate shop") plus 1–2 of the interest tags, the business's own words as tags, and **its next dated thing this week or next** (a class, a tasting, a sale) where its site states one.
3. **The PM sees before and after on You** ("To approve"), visible to him alone, and taps Approve, Reject or Edit, with a feedback box.
4. **Approve** replaces the live Page's fields; **Reject** leaves the Page as it was. The agent reads the PM's feedback before its next picks.
5. **New businesses** come the same way, as drafts on You, never straight onto Explore (the PM: "I need samples to approve").

## Settled in the conversation

- **Instagram and other platforms: links only, never read for tags.** Automated collection breaks Instagram's terms and the PM's 2026-10-16 rule (THEMES.md step 6–7). Tags come from the business's own site; the PM may add platform-style tags in his feedback. He accepted this ("go").
- **Contact emails never go in a public repo** (socialus-web is public). They live where only the PM sees them.
- **Feedback starts as a text box;** a chat with the in-app assistant is a later step.

## Precedents
