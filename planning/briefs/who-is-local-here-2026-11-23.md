## The picture

Ana looks at a bakery's Page. Under About: a photo of two hands in flour and "I'm Lupe. I live in Woodland and I've baked here since 2009. I started because my grandmother's conchas had no shop." A month later there's a new line: why the oven is named Beto. On a Saturday in Grass Valley, Ana looks from there and the Pages lead with the people who live there. A bookstore whose owner added nothing looks just as it does today. Someone who shares Lupe's values sees a blue dot by her name; nobody else could tell there was one.

## Options: the first slice

**Tiny, by January 6, with any pick: words only.** No new screen or data: the vendor email asks owners to say who they are; the About box's hint asks for it ("who you are and why you do this", a placeholder until you write it). About an hour, once your words are in. It holds nothing on the must-have line.

- **A. The owner's card (recommended).** Each Page owner may add a card about **themself**: the name they go by, one photo if they like, the town they live or work in, since when, and up to three short answers in their own words (*why I do this · what I love about this town · what I wish people knew*; your words to come). All optional; nothing nags. The town line replaces the retired "Claimed local owner". About 25 hours [A]. The thing you asked for, in its smallest honest form.
- **B. A, plus the story keeps going.** A quiet prompt a few times a year ("what's new with you?"), answers kept as a short timeline on the card, a photo each, and it reaches people who get updates. Makes it part of owning a Page. About 25 hours more [A], and it needs screening like any post.
- **C. B, plus finding by people.** "Meet who runs it" as an Explore shelf and an answer to "who can I support here?", at home or from the vantage when travelling; the neighbor line on a Page. About 30 hours more [A]. Puts faces where people choose, which is where the bias risk below is.

**Why A first:** five to ten real owners writing a card tells us whether people read them, before we build the habit around it. B follows when ten owners have written one [A].

## Privacy: no stalking

- **The person writes it about themself.** Nobody can add, tag or name anyone else, and a photo of someone else needs their yes.
- **Never:** home address, neighbourhood, zip, legal name, age, schedule, where they are now, or a link to their account or their other Pages. The finest place is a town they type.
- **Members only by default;** one globe or lock on the card lets an owner show everyone (the 10-07 contact pattern). Showing it to visitors would amend F134's "member names stay between members" for this card only; your yes to A includes it.
- **Not a way to find a person:** names and photos aren't searchable, sortable or on result cards. No public member profile returns (10-01).
- **Off in one step.** Reported words hide the card, not the Page. Photos are screened like every photo.
- **Unclaimed Pages never carry one,** and we never write one for an owner.

**The honest risk: faces invite bias.** Harvard researchers found Black Airbnb hosts earned about 12% less than others for like listings [A: search summary; the paper refused the fetch], and field tests found bias by name. So a photo is optional, can be a shop or a pair of hands, and stays inside the Page, off result cards.

## With values, and the blue dot (CO-0068)

- **The story is public; the survey answers stay private,** and never mix: the card asks nothing about politics or faith, and the survey never fills the card.
- **The dot would sit by the owner's name,** lit only for an aligned viewer who took the survey; everyone else gets the same card with nothing missing.
- **Writing a card never counts as taking the survey,** and not writing one never lowers a Page.

## Your patronage supports a neighbor

- **Said as the owner's fact, not our arithmetic:** "Run by Lupe, who lives in Woodland" [placeholder]. No "near you", no miles, the same from every vantage (F106).
- **When travelling (F105):** looking from Grass Valley leads with the people who live there. It already fits "support the people who live where you are visiting".
- **No money claim until one is true.** "Your money stays here" waits for give-back. The reason to want it: in Salt Lake City, independent shops returned 52% of revenue locally against 14% for chains, and restaurants 79% against 30% ([Civic Economics, in ILSR's summary](https://ilsr.org/articles/key-studies-why-local-matters/)); shares vary by city.
- **Neighbors can vouch** (the 10-27 vouching idea) is a separate piece: theirs, not the owner's.

## Where the money goes (later; nothing to decide now)

Kept together so it isn't lost: a Page owner names the community their give-back goes to (F106, approved for later); give-back goes only to a legal entity the community forms (2026-10-12); how a town and that entity meet is the open question in `places.md`; a seller's chosen fund (CO-0031), local is where the money returns (CO-0039), places compete to keep spending local (CO-0027), and where a business's money goes, in levels (plan PR #364). Tax and structure are socialus-legal's. The card leaves one empty line where it would show.

## Precedents

| Precedent | What it does | Take or leave |
|---|---|---|
| [Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628487-How-to-Edit-Your-Shop-s-About-Section) | A shop story, five photos, and each team member's name, role, bio and photo on the About page | Take: people on the Page, one card each |
| [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3386) | All profile details optional, but a host photo is required and can show in search | Take optional; leave the required photo and search |
| [Kickstarter](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236550-what-information-can-be-found-in-the-about-the-creator-section) | Verified name, location, bio, last login, record of projects | Take bio and place; leave last login (stalking) and scores |
| Faire "Meet the maker", farmers' market vendor bios | A maker's story beside the goods | Take the idea; **not read** (the sites refused or weren't reached) |
| [Nextdoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nextdoor) | Real neighbors by verified address | Leave: our proof is the phone, our place is a town |

**Does it work?** A study of 1,800 Etsy seller pages linked a profile describing the seller to more sales, most of all words about the person's "being" ([Portnoy and Raban, 2022](https://cris.haifa.ac.il/en/publications/personal-profile-management-on-etsy-2/)); wary students preferred an unknown shop that showed a person or building ([Darke, 2016](https://phys.org/news/2016-08-colonel-sanders-business-owner-reassure.html)). Linked, not proven cause; neither is a local app.

## Five Deadly Sins, plainly

- **Unrealistic schedule:** nothing but words before January 6. A lands in late January or February, after the opening settles.
- **Scope creep:** this is a theme with three parts; A is one. Money, vouching and the survey stay out.
- **Gold plating:** no video, no interviews, no badges, no verified names.
- **Vague requirements:** the three questions are yours to write; the hours are estimates.
- **Unclear acceptance:** the criteria above; success is owners writing cards and people opening them, counted per market, ten or more.
