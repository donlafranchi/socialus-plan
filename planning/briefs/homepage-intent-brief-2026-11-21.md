## What a first-time visitor should feel in a few seconds

1. **Get it.** They understand what SocialUs is.
2. **Feel invited to try.** Search comes first and nothing competes with it.
3. **Sense it's specific and personal.**
4. **Sense their searches matter** and can bring things here.
5. **Feel it's different, and trust it a little.** Said as intentions, with the one fixed thing being no extraction.
6. **Know there's more** if they want it.

## How it should feel

Clean, calm, warm, neighborly, hopeful, uncluttered and confident. Never salesy.
