## Checked against the code, 2026-11-05

The copywriter's review ("a copy review, not verification") asked for the promises to be confirmed. What changed and why:

- **Reports.** "You hear why and get to answer first" held only for a first, ordinary report. A report of Threat of harm or Sensitive content hides the post at once; for Sensitive content the poster isn't told at all (ruled 2026-10-07). The AI can put a post back in live mode but never removes one, so "Only a person can remove it" replaces "A person decides".
- **Posts coming down.** Every kind ends, but "comes down" overstated it: a post leaves Explore and search and stays on the Page under Earlier. Undated Updates go after 7 days (not 14), Limited and Proposals after 14, jobs after 30; dated ones when they end.
- **Proposal.** The button reads "I'm in" ("Count me in" shows only when you pick a size). Everyone who can see the post sees the count, the names of who's in, and a bar if a goal was set.
- **Claiming.** Signed in, it's three steps: Claim this Page, your email at the website's address, then the code from the email. Owner at once, no staff step. "It takes a minute" depends on the email arriving, so it's out.
- **Who gets updates.** An owner sees that count only inside the composer's audience switch, under an option that isn't working yet, so the count is cut and "who hearted your posts" stays.
- **How it works, step 3.** Publishing asks for a name, where it is and a description; a photo is optional and a first post isn't a step.
- **Unchanged and true:** no charge to create a Page; the post kinds; get updates, heart, save, Remind me and Add to calendar; Pages started from a business's own website; nothing paid in search; the emailed 6-digit code with no password; the copy-link block.
