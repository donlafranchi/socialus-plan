# The town before it opens

**As of 2026-11-21** · Source: the PM's words below; Two sides: Find and Discover (CO-0035), Asked for, not here yet (CO-0043), F118, F134, F136, the unmet-search and welcome brief, the waitlist journey brief, the producer count rule (D-0402), privacy means no stalking (2026-11-15) · Maintained by: hand (brief; deleted at 30 days or once ruled and placed)

*The PM, 2026-11-21:* "I see this app as a discovery tool and while that's necessary and useful, I don't think it's very engaging so I'm trying to think of ways to make it engaging and then the second topic was about the waitlist people and signing up in basically creating their community through their searches or conversations"

**Bottom line: let people waiting for their town search now, and turn what they search into wishes that float up the screen like live-stream hearts; a tap says "me too". Ship search plus floating wishes first (B); the town board waits until moderation can carry it.**

**Your call:** which ships first for towns that aren't open yet: A, B or C?

## The picture

Maya in Vacaville joined the waitlist. She opens SocialUs and searches "pottery class". Nothing local yet: *"Every search tells local makers what Vacaville wants."* She taps **Me too**, adds the studio in Dixon she loves (an unclaimed Page ready to claim), and watches Vacaville's wishes drift up the screen: *sourdough · pottery class · kids' climbing · farm stand*. She taps ♡ on farm stand. The town page shows 41 locals, 6 makers, top wishes, and how close Vacaville is to opening. When it opens, there's a welcome, and everyone who said "me too" to pottery hears the studio is here.

## Options

- **A. Search and wishes, still.** Waitlisted people search; told why; "me too" and add-a-place on nothing-found; the town page lists top wishes and progress. Smallest and safest; not much more engaging than today.
- **B. A plus floating wishes (recommended).** Top wishes float bottom to top, tap ♡ for "me too". Gives the PM's moment of "what is my town asking for" with no new member text to moderate. Needs a threshold ruling (below); a new town's float is thin until enough people search.
- **C. B plus a town board.** Ask a neighbor, ideas and wishes in words, so community forms in conversation. Closest to the PM's second topic; every post needs the report path, screening and the one-operator review load (D-0158), so it is the most to build and run.

## What's safe to float (the new territory)

- **Only known things:** a search floats as the item tag or category it maps to ("pottery class"), never the words typed. Unknown, sensitive or unmapped searches never float.
- **Only enough people:** a wish floats once enough different people in that market searched it. Producers already see a term only at ten (D-0402); the float is public, so it should not be lower. Default ten, the PM may lower it for wishes that are categories only.
- **Never live, never anyone:** a few minutes' delay, no names, per market, no "someone near you just searched". That keeps it on the right side of no stalking.
- **Bounded:** a set of top wishes plays and stops. It is a check-in, not a feed (works for you; the north star is time and money, not attention).

## Precedents

- **Floating reactions:** [Periscope's hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periscope_(service)) and [Facebook Live Reactions, April 2016](https://fortune.com/2016/04/06/facebook-beefs-up-live-video/): tap and it floats up in real time. [Facebook changed it in July 2016](https://thenextweb.com/news/facebook-updates-live) after emoji flew over hard-hitting videos: tone needs a guard.
- **Live search tickers, 1990s:** [MetaCrawler's MetaSpy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MetaCrawler) ran a filtered view beside an unfiltered "Exposed" one; [Excite's Search Voyeur](https://anildash.com/1999/10/12/search-engine-q/) streamed raw searches; Ask Jeeves had "Peek Through the Keyhole". Raw queries were offensive and revealing, so the filtered versions won and the tickers faded. [AOL's 2006 search log release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AOL_search_log_release) showed searches alone can identify a person.
- **Aggregated interest:** [Google Trends](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533) shows only terms with enough volume, sampled and normalized; low-volume terms read zero.
- **Founding neighbours:** [Nextdoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nextdoor) opened a neighbourhood once ten households joined; the founding member named it and invited neighbours.
- **Waitlists with something to do:** [Clubhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clubhouse_(app)) let waitlisted people reserve a name and be pulled in by friends; [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) "coming soon" pages gather followers before launch.
- **Community before the product:** [Midjourney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midjourney) and many games ran their whole early community in Discord or a subreddit before an app existed.
