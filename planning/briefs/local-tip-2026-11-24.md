# Local tip: the third kind of post without a Page

## The change

- **Retire `ask`.** Composer, lead line and search stop offering it; any live `ask` post is withdrawn; the kind check and shelf-life table drop the value (one migration, applied in order).
- **Add `tip`: Local tip.** The composer asks three things: *which place or Page* (the existing place typeahead; Pages by name), *what about it* (one short line: the patio, the Tuesday lunch, the back room), and *the tip* in the member's words. Hint (draft): "One specific thing locals should know about a place." Placeholder (draft): "The patio at Ruhstaller takes dogs and has shade by 2." Lead line: `Local tip · Ruhstaller · patio`. Seven days is wrong for a tip that stays true: no expiry while the place is open; the author can withdraw it; a tip about a Page that closes ends with it. Counted in the three-at-once cap.
- **Specific, not whole-place.** "What about it" is required and short; a tip whose "what" is the place's name or "everything" is refused with a line saying what is missing. The AI that already reads member posts (F100) can flag a whole-place tip for the author to narrow, never block it on its own.
- **Love.** One tap per member per tip; the author sees the count; nothing adds loves up on the place or Page. Draft mark and word for the PM to see live.
- **Where it shows.** Nowhere on its own. The chat or search answer (F119's Ask bar now, F120's chat later; F136's search) includes the tips relevant to the ask, each attached to the place or Page it is about, ranked by relevance, then detail and specificity, then loves. A Page's screen also lists its tips, most loved first, under its posts. The owner can report a tip, not remove it.
- **Never a review.** No stars, score, thumbs or average on a place; a love is on the tip. The 2026-11-01 warn waits.
- **In the knowledge store.** The brief names its concept (`concept: new`), so the import creates the Local tip concept with this as its first change; the rulings import from `nouns.md` and `discovery.md`. Tips themselves are member content in `member_posts`, not store records; the store holds what a tip *is*, so the agent can answer "what do locals say about the patio?" from the app's data under the ruled shape.

**Path: new territory** (member trust: a tip about a named Page is a member speaking about a business in public; loves are a public signal). Precedents: [Nextdoor recommendations](https://help.nextdoor.com/s/article/About-Recommendations) (a neighbour's post about a business, no stars); [Google Local Guides](https://support.google.com/local-guides/answer/6225846) (free-text tips apart from reviews, surfaced inside the place's listing and in answers); [Foursquare tips](https://foursquare.com/) (one specific line per tip, "the patio is the point", ranked by upvotes, never a star); [Yelp Tips](https://www.yelp-support.com/article/What-is-a-tip) (retired 2023 because they sat beside reviews; here the tip is the only member voice about a Page).
