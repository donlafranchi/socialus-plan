# Local tip: the third kind of post without a Page

**As of 2026-11-24** · Source: the PM's words below; `planning/research/posts-people-and-pages-2026-11-02.md` (option B, ruled plan #270); web #1184 (`src/lib/member-posts/kinds.ts`, `supabase/migrations/20261105180000_member_posts.sql`), read 2026-11-24; the 2026-11-01 rulings in `product/systems/discovery.md` and `product/foundation/nouns.md` · Maintained by: hand (brief; deleted at 30 days or once ruled and placed)

*The PM, 2026-11-24:* "Give me the A/B/C again. Also ask a neighbor is the wrong side of the coin. Although I like the other two as they are." *Then:* "b".

*Earlier, 2026-11-01:* "most importantly we need people to be able to share happenings and insights, recommendations and local tips" … "I don't want this to be a review site" … "when many locals have a bad experience with a business or page they can 'warn' other locals. This is peer pressure for good behavior hopefully. This must work both ways."

**Bottom line: option B is already built (web #1184): a member can post without a Page in three kinds, Lost & found, Free / for sale and Ask a neighbor, gated behind the phone check. The PM keeps the first two and retires the third: asking is the member needing something, and the kind he wants is the member *giving* something. Swap "Ask a neighbor" for a Local tip, a recommendation or a happening in the member's own words, that can point at the Page or place it is about. No review, no rating, no warn yet.**

**Your call:** one word and one layout, below. Everything else is decided.

## The change

- **Retire `ask`.** The composer, lead line, Explore row and search group stop offering it. The one live test post of that kind, if any, is withdrawn; the column check and the shelf-life table drop the value (one migration).
- **Add `tip`: Local tip.** Hint (draft): "Something good you found, or something happening, that locals should know." Placeholder (draft): "The taqueria on 16th does a $5 lunch Tuesdays. Line moves fast." Lead line: `Local tip · Today · Midtown`. Seven days. One answer in place of comments, draft "Good to know" (today's "I'm interested" reads wrong on a tip).
- **About a Page or place.** Optional: the member names the Page or place the tip is about (the same typeahead the yard-sale street uses for places; Pages by name). A tip that names a Page shows on that Page under its posts as well; the Page's owner cannot remove it, only report it. Ruled 2026-11-01: "on the Page or place it is about".
- **Never a review.** No stars, no score, no thumbs, no "N tips about this Page". The 2026-11-01 ruling holds; the warn waits.

**Path: new territory** (member trust: a tip about a named Page is a member speaking about a business in public). Precedents: [Nextdoor recommendations](https://help.nextdoor.com/s/article/About-Recommendations) are a post kind a neighbour writes about a business, with no stars; [Google Local Guides](https://support.google.com/local-guides/answer/6225846) post tips as free text separately from reviews; [Yelp Tips](https://www.yelp-support.com/article/What-is-a-tip) were one-line notes beside reviews and were retired in 2023, which is why the tip here is the only member voice about a Page, not a second one.

## Two things for the PM

**The word.** A: **Local tip** (recommended: says who is speaking and matches "locals supporting locals"). B: **Recommendation** (Nextdoor's word; narrower, loses the happening). C: **Tip** (shortest; reads like a gratuity on a shop Page).

**The layout.** Today member posts sit in their own rows ("From neighbors"); the 2026-11-01 ruling says a locals tip is "mixed in with Pages' posts" with a search filter for all / Pages' posts / tips. A: **keep the rows as built, and add the filter in search** (recommended: fits the same-day ruling that a row holds one type, web #1154, and needs no new shelf logic). B: **mix tips into the post shelves** as ruled, with the filter; a reader cannot tell a Page's post from a person's at a glance, which the 2026-11-02 research warned against. Whichever is chosen retires the other sentence in `product/systems/discovery.md`.

**The "neighbor" word** on these surfaces (the heading, `/neighbors`) is a separate word call the briefing already flags; not needed for this change.

## What it leaves alone

The Lost & found and Free / for sale kinds, their lives, answers and words; the phone gate; the warn (parked until there are tips to count); a Proposal without a Page (parked); any public member profile (ruled out).
