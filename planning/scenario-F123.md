## Story

*The PM, 2026-10-31, verbatim:* "What if we collect some info from the page creators or have them create a page and show that on the Dashboard as a kind of free advertising for their market? Also what about people not clearly in a market? Essentially have them create a page early and it'll be ready to go when the market opens."

A baker in Dixon creates her Page while Dixon is still waiting. She picks what she makes and adds her site and Instagram. Her Page appears under "Makers already here" on her market's page, grouped by kind, and counts toward its 100 makers. The day the market opens, it is live.

## Acceptance

1. A maker anywhere can create a Page now. The zip puts it in its market (F103); a county nobody has grouped is its own waiting market, so no one is outside a market. If the market is redrawn, the Page follows.
2. The maker gives a kind and their site or socials, the same intake as F118.
3. The market page lists its makers, grouped by kind (food, shops, makers, services, groups and events), plus a "Still missing" line naming kinds with none.
4. Each Page counts once toward the 100 makers, and the maker can remove it any time (the existing remove path).
5. Until the market opens, the Page shows only on its market's page, not elsewhere in the app.

## Not this

Payments, paid placement, or showing waiting Pages in other markets.

[open-question owner=don raised=2026-11-01] Is "only on its market's page until open" right, or should the Page be reachable by link too?

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** No: Kickstarter creator pages and Nextdoor founding businesses exist, but not a Page ready for the day a market opens.
- **Only locals know?** Yes: which makers a place has is local knowledge.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: makers and supporters see each other before opening.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: counts follow Pages as they are made or removed.
- **Who keeps it current?** The makers themselves.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: each Page makes the market more worth joining.

Soul: `#out-of-the-way`, `#you-choose`. **Path: well-worn.** Precedents: Kickstarter creator pages, Nextdoor founding businesses, Airbnb host sign-up ahead of a city launch.
