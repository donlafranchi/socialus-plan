## Story

A developer in Davis, a neighbour who likes to organise, and a designer who is out of work each open Join the team. They see what kinds of help the platform wants, what is paid and what is volunteer, and they leave a note saying what they could do and how to reach them. One person at SocialUs reads each note; they get an answer.

## Acceptance

Backlog; not approved. The shape so far:

1. **The page lists the roles that exist today, in plain words.** Starting set for the PM to confirm: building (software, design), community (moderation help, local outreach), and operations. Each says whether it is paid or volunteer. Roles are one short list edited by the operator, not a job board.
2. **Anyone can leave an interest note, signed in or not:** name, an email, which role, a few lines about what they could do. Nothing else is asked.
3. **Interest notes are staff-side only.** No member or visitor sees another's note. They are kept only as long as needed to answer and are deleted on request (DECISIONS 2026-10-09: staff get the data they need under roles and permissions).
4. **A note is answered by a person from our team** (one operator today; the standing rule is members self-correct before staff work, so the page sets expectations on reply time and takes no promise to answer every note).
5. **Held for `socialus-legal`, not designed here:** employment or contractor terms, equity or any share of income promised to a contributor (members are the investors and the only people paid out, SETTLED), volunteer liability and any payment. The page makes no offer of pay beyond "paid" or "volunteer" per role.

## Not this

A job board, an application tracker, a resume upload, interviews in the app, a public list of who applied, or any promise of pay or ownership.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** No: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Get_involved) has a get-involved page; ours is shorter and tied to our roles.
- **Only locals know?** Partly: local outreach needs local people.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: it turns members who want to help into a team.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Mostly: the role list is a handful of lines reviewed by the operator.
- **Who keeps it current?** The operator, with the roadmap.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: more members, more help.

**Path: new territory.** It collects personal data from visitors and touches employment and ownership questions that go to `socialus-legal`. Nothing is decided about pay or equity.
