## Story

*The PM, 2026-10-31, verbatim:* "Founders aren't the first people to sign up, they are the first people to get others to sign up. I think Yelp Elite is a good model too. We also want people to spread the word so maybe there are a few recognition badges. I'm thinking of people on TikTok who post videos about other businesses and talk about the business and spread awareness." *And:* "If I am an early person and I add an account that's prolific then I had a great impact early on. The early ones are critical even if they aren't the most by quantity. ... It could literally mean them becoming more influential members of their communities not just a badge on an app."

Mara in Davis shares her link with a ceramicist, who brings in a dozen makers. Months later Davis's page names Mara a Founder of Davis, because most of the market grew from her one early invite. She opted in to being named.

## Acceptance

1. **One source:** recognition reads only the invite log ruled in F118 (2026-10-31). Nothing else is tracked for it.
2. **Impact is the chain, not the count:** a member's effect is everyone downstream of their invites, with early invites weighted. The weighting is open; the log keeps what any weighting needs.
3. **A few badges, per market, kept long term:** Founder (brought people in), Matchmaker (added makers who then claimed their Pages), Voice (shared links that led to visits and sign-ups). Names and thresholds are open.
4. **A Movers & Shakers board per market**, on its page and the dashboard. A name shows only if the member opted in; otherwise counts only.
5. **Voice** is measured by anonymous totals per link: bots filtered, no visitor identity stored.
6. Test accounts never count, and each person counts once.

## Not this

Cash, credit or perks (ruled out, F118 2026-10-31). Naming anyone who did not opt in. Ranking Pages.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** No: Yelp Elite and Nextdoor Leads reward activity, but not who started a market.
- **Only locals know?** Yes: only neighbours know who really got their community going.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: it pays people for bringing neighbours together.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: the board moves with sign-ups and claims.
- **Who keeps it current?** Nobody; it is computed from the invite log.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: every invite lengthens someone's chain.
