## Story

Before soft launch the PM opens one card and sees, bar by bar, whether SocialUs does what its pitch says: an owner's special lasts until it ends, a new member sees what is on without searching, what they gave (follows, interests, area) changes what they see, and a Page says who is behind it. Each bar is pass or fail on production. Derived from the pitch [derives pitch@2026-10-16] (`PRINCIPLES.md#pitch`, audit `planning/research/pitch-audit-2026-10-15.md`).

## Acceptance

Each bar opens with the capability it grades. All are drafts for the PM to keep, loosen or tighten.

1. `cap-1` An owner posts a dated special in at most 8 taps; it shows from posting until it ends, and can span days.
2. `cap-2` A new member with a zip sees at least 6 things happening this week on first open, with 0 taps.
3. `cap-3` Nothing the member did not give orders their results; at least one signal they gave (follows or interests) visibly changes what they see.
4. `cap-4` From You, in at most 3 taps, a member sets or clears their area and interests.
5. `cap-5` A member following up to 5 Pages learns this week's posts from all of them without opening each Page.
6. `cap-6` Before posting, an owner sees who will see it and where; a claim can be completed.
7. `cap-7` On a phone, signed out, the PM can find a Page and see what is on and when, as on Yelp or Google.
8. `cap-8` From any card, a member sees who is behind a Page (story and links) in 1 tap, and can vouch for it or report a false claim in 2. Measured on the dashboard as Page-level totals only: Pages with an owner-written story or linked socials, vouches per Page, false-claim reports upheld.

## Not this

Ranking by engagement, vouches or payment. Per-member tracking on the card. A weekly email (the audit's question for the PM, not decided here). A badge for every origin.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** No: no other app grades itself against a local-first promise.
- **Only locals know?** Partly: whether this area's Pages say what is on is local; the bars are not.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: bars 1, 2 and 5 are about what is on and when.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: the card is read from production each time, not kept by hand.
- **Who keeps it current?** The dashboard reads it; `[derives pitch@date]` flags it when the pitch moves.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: bars 3 and 8 improve with more follows and vouches.

**Path: new territory** (the PM sets the bars). Precedents for a pass/fail launch card: Google's Core Web Vitals thresholds, Apple's App Store review checklist.
