## Story

*The PM, 2026-11-26, verbatim:* "I want themes in the store. and journeys associated with one (and potentially more) themes since they can touch multiple like the 'open your town'."

The PM wonders how SocialUs rewards members for growing the platform. He asks for that theme. The answer lists every journey it touches (a neighbour brings in a maker they love; a market opens; the person who brought them is recognised), the concepts and features on each, and what is built. Open your town sits under that theme and under the one for towns that run themselves, and shows under both.

## Acceptance

1. **A theme is a record:** a name, the PM's words for why it matters, and its journeys. It has no steps of its own; the steps live on its journeys (F132).
2. **Many to many:** a journey may belong to several themes, and a theme holds several journeys. Open your town can sit under more than one.
3. **Up and down in one call:** asking about a theme returns its journeys, their built state and the open questions on them; asking about a journey or concept returns every theme it sits under.
4. **A new idea finds its theme:** the "where does this fit" answer (F132.5) names the themes as well as the journey, or says "no theme yet".
5. **First themes are real:** "Members grow the platform" (F118, F122, vouching, local scouts, reciprocity) and "Who is local here?" (JY-LOCAL) load with their journeys.
6. **Short answers:** each answer fits a phone screen, in plain words, no ticket numbers.

## Not this

Drawing themes as a picture. Ranking or scoring themes. Rewriting any feature's scope. Deciding which rewards exist: that stays with the PM, one idea at a time.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** No: today a theme is a phrase in a brief, so it can't be asked about and goes stale when the brief is deleted.
- **Only locals know?** No: it is how the product is planned.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: the first theme is members bringing neighbours in.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: journeys and their built state already update themselves (F132).
- **Who keeps it current?** Agents attach journeys to themes as ideas arrive; the PM rules on the themes.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: a new person sees why a feature exists in one call.

**Path: well-worn.** Themes grouping epics or journeys: [Jira's themes, initiatives and epics](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/project-management/epics-stories-themes) and [Patton's story mapping](https://jpattonassociates.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/story_mapping.pdf), where a theme spans several maps.
