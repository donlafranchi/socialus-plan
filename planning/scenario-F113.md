## Story

Don asks SocialUs for bikes and gives a thumbs up to a steel single speed for sale, then over two weeks to four more steel single speeds, a hardtail mountain bike and a beach cruiser. He never manages anything. Behind the scenes each post carries labels SocialUs gave it (bicycle · single speed · steel frame · used · Midtown). In You a line appears: "Seems you're into: steel single speeds". He taps "That's me". His next bike answers lead with steel single speeds. Bowling, from a few thumbs up for a friend, he marks "Not really", and it doesn't come back.

## Acceptance

1. **A thumbs up on every card** (Explore, search, Page, post, chat answers) meaning "more like this". One tap, tap again to undo. It isn't a save, notifies no one and subscribes to nothing.
2. **Labels behind the scenes:** every Page, post and listing gets labels when it's posted or edited, from one small model call on its words and photos: what it is (broad to narrow, from the item-tag dictionary, #1318), material or style, the way it's offered (sell, trade, teach, gather, repair, lend), new, used or handmade, price band. Where and when come from the post. The owner sees and can fix them; no member ever types one, and no label shows on a card.
3. **Thumbs up are never listed for the member to manage.** Each one counts toward the labels of what was tapped, and fades: strong while taps keep coming, gone if they stop (start at a 60-day half-life [guess], tune in beta).
4. **The summary in You:** "Seems you're into", a short list, shown only once enough agree (start at 5 thumbs up sharing a label over 2 or more days [guess]). Each line: "That's me" or "Not really". "Not really" removes it and it isn't guessed back; "That's me" holds it steady.
5. **What it changes:** Discover, Home shelves (F121) and the Ask bar's answers lean toward the summary and recent thumbs up. Find's plain order and search relevance do not change.
6. **One switch off** ("I don't mind doing this myself") stops learning and deletes the thumbs up and the summary.
7. **Nobody else sees it:** no member, Page owner or visitor can read anyone's thumbs up or summary; a test proves it, seen failing first. No Page learns who gave it one. Builder accounts count nowhere (F114).
8. **Never from** opens, views, time spent or scrolling.
9. **Two weekly numbers on the PM's dashboard:** the share of summary lines marked "That's me", and how often an answer after a thumbs up leads to an act (saved, messaged, signed up, bought). Counts only.

## Not this

A list of likes; a public like count; hashtags on cards; "Not this", "How was it?" and similar-member matching (later, in that order); ads or anything sold.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** No: general apps know "bicycling", not the steel single speed a neighbor is selling.
- **Only locals know?** Yes: what's offered nearby, and who wants it.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: buyers find sellers, learners find teachers.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: thumbs up fade; nobody tends them.
- **Who keeps it current?** The member, by tapping; SocialUs, by labelling.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: more posts to label and match.

**Path: new territory, ruled.** Precedents: Netflix thumbs, Pinterest's interest labels and Refine your recommendations, Spotify's taste profile with Exclude (brief § The button). Soul: `#works-for-you`, `#evolve-with-members`, `#you-choose`.
