## Story

A member has searched "trivia" twice and said "I'm coming" to three trivia nights. Home asks once: "Add trivia to your interests?" They tap Add, and Home's rows start carrying trivia nights. In You, "What SocialUs knows about you" lists their interests, follows, RSVPs and recent searches; they delete one search. Later they turn off "Learn from what I do": suggestions stop, the Explore open log stops, and the raw signals are gone. Explore looks the same as before throughout.

## Acceptance

Not written. Brought to the PM first (options below); criteria follow the call. Fixed under every option:

- **Explore's order, search relevance, what another member sees and what a Page owner sees about a member do not change.**
- **Every signal is listed to the member and deletable one at a time.** One switch stops all learning, including the F109 log, and deletes raw signals.
- **Raw signals (searches, opens, RSVPs past their date) expire after 90 days on their own** [guess]. No operator step.
- **Builder accounts' signals count nowhere** (F114).

## Not this

Learning on Explore (F112). Ads or anything sold. A score or label on a person. Telling another member what someone searched. Sharing signals outside SocialUs.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** Partly: platforms show what they learned; few let the member decide what it may change.
- **Only locals know?** Yes: what a member looks for nearby is local.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: Home leads with gatherings the member actually goes to.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: signals expire on their own.
- **Who keeps it current?** The member.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: more to suggest from.

**Path: new territory** (member trust, privacy). Research: `planning/research/explore-freshness-and-learning-2026-10-12.md` § 2.

**For the PM (A/B/C):**
- A) **Declared, with suggestions the member accepts** (recommended): signals only suggest interests; Home ranks on what the member declared and follows. Fits the 2026-09-12 and 2026-10-04 rulings as written. Goodreads, Threads, Kay's scrutable models.
- B) **Inferred interests, shown and editable**: Home ranks on interests the platform infers, each with its reason. Best relevance (Pinterest, Spotify, Google); needs those two rulings changed, not the Charter.
- C) **Follows only**: no learning; recent searches stay in the browser. Nothing to explain; Home and Explore feel alike.
