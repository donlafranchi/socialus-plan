## Story

A member has asked for bikes twice and tapped "More like this" on five steel single speeds over two weeks. In You, "Seems you're into" shows Steel single speeds, from 2 asks and 5 taps. They mark Bowling, from a few taps for a friend, "Not really", and it does not come back. Later they switch to "I don't mind doing this myself": learning stops and what was kept is deleted. Find looks the same throughout.

## Acceptance

1. **A profile the member sees, from beta**: in You, every interest SocialUs holds for the member, each with its reason in plain counts ("from 2 asks, 5 taps"), and beside it their follows, "I'm coming", saves, recent searches and what they have been shown (F112).
2. **Every item can be edited or deleted one at a time.** A removed interest is not inferred again unless the member adds it back. An interest the member adds is kept as theirs.
3. **The choice in the member's terms**: "Help me discover things I might like" (on, as the data ruling has it) or "I don't mind doing this myself" (off). Off stops all learning and F112's seen record, and deletes raw signals; what the member declared and follows stays. Words to the PM.
4. **What it learns from**: what the member asks and searches; and what they do on purpose: "More like this" taps (kept quietly, fading, never listed for the member to manage), saves, Remind me, "I'm coming", messaging, signing up, buying, "not for me", follows. Never opens, views, time spent or scrolling (2026-11-20 ruling). Each is turned into interests through SocialUs's own categories (research note § 8), not member-typed hashtags.
5. **What it may change in beta**: which interests the profile shows, and F112's not-seen-yet. Find's place-and-time order and search relevance do not change. After beta, Discover and Home read the same profile.
6. **Raw signals expire after 90 days** [guess]; interests stay until removed or until no signal supports them for 90 days. No operator step.
7. **Nobody else sees it.** Member to member strict; staff under the 2026-10-09 ruling, with no tool that targets one member. Builder accounts' signals count nowhere (F114).

## Not this

Ads or anything sold. Telling another member what someone searched. Hashtags on cards. Using collected data for anyone's profit but the members' own.

## Why

- **Lives somewhere better?** Partly: platforms show what they learned; few let the member decide what it may change.
- **Only locals know?** Yes: what a member looks for nearby is local.
- **Gets people together?** Yes: what they like leads them to gatherings.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Yes: signals expire on their own.
- **Who keeps it current?** The member.
- **Better as more people join?** Yes: more to learn from.

**Path: new territory, ruled.** Precedents: Pinterest's Refine your recommendations, Spotify's Exclude from your taste profile, TikTok's Refresh (research note § 3). **Conflict, noted for the two-sides review:** reads behaviour back at the member (F059, 2026-09-12).

## Rulings
- **2026-11-25 — Get specific: recommendations learn taste at the level a member cares about (steel single speeds, not bicycling). Every card carries "More like this"; a tap narrows the answer now and is kept quietly as a fading signal the member never has to manage. Once enough taps agree, You shows a short summary of the member's taste, which they confirm or fix, and the summary leans what they are shown. The record also learns from asks and deliberate acts (saves, Remind me, "I'm coming", messaging, signing up, buying, follows, "not for me"); never from opens, views, time spent or scrolling. "How was it?" is a later, small extra; "I went" is dropped. Ask-matching comes before similar-member matching, which switches on per market only once it beats it, with ten or more members behind any pattern.** The PM, verbatim: *"the name is get specific. as far as the button I want the method to be adaptable over time. one time is not enough. the button creates a taste profile. the taste profile is more like a summary once there is enough to go on. I essentially want the likes or "show me more like this" button to be something the user doesn't need to manage and perhaps is ephemeral and always evolving."* Because a member should get results as specific as they are without tending a list.
