# Post once, show everywhere: helping businesses without more work (2026-10-31)

**As of 2026-10-31** · Source: hand-written research, product lane · Path: new territory (member trust, copyright, platform terms) · Research only; nothing here is ruled. · Maintained by: hand (the owner confirms on next edit)

**Bottom line: a business should never post to SocialUs. We already read venues' own events pages and turn their shows into dated posts with credit; widen that same reader to every unclaimed Page's website, calendar feed, blog feed and product feed, keep only the facts (kind · when · where, a link back), and label them Sample until the owner claims. After a claim, one tap connects Instagram, YouTube and Shopify so their own words and photos flow in with consent.**

Google and Yelp build unclaimed listings from data and invite a claim; posting tools (Later, Buffer) and store channels (Shopify, Square) work only once the owner connects.**

## What we can read, and what we can't

**Without a login we may read what a business publishes for machines to read: event data on its own site, calendar and blog feeds, a public YouTube feed, and (gently) a store's product feed. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok are off limits until they claim and connect; scraping them breaks their terms.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

**Without their login (before a claim)**

| Source | OK? | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Event data on their own site (structured data, the same thing Google reads) | Yes | Facts only; already built |
| Calendar feeds (iCal) | Yes | Facts only; already built |
| Blog / news feed (RSS, Atom), Substack feed | Yes | Title, date, link; not the full text |
| YouTube channel feed or Data API | Yes | Attribution; limits on how long data is kept (verify before building) |
| Shopify public product feed | Uncertain | No Shopify policy clearly allows it; some stores switch it off. Rate-limit and cache, or wait for consent |
| Shopify's agent commerce endpoint (Plant Foundry has one) | Likely yes | Built for agents to discover products; newest route, worth a test |
| Newsletter, by subscribing a SocialUs inbox | Facts only | Subscribing is allowed; the text and photos are theirs |
| Ticketmaster Discovery API | Yes | Free key, attribution and link-back; for arena shows (verify limits) |
| Eventbrite | Partly | Public search [ended in 2019–20](https://groups.google.com/g/eventbrite-api/c/FT2MsDswdrA); lookup by known organizer still works |
| Meetup | No | Pro only |
| Google Business posts | No | Owner only |
| Instagram, Facebook, TikTok | **No** | Scraping signed in breaks their terms. A court let logged-off scraping of public data stand in [Meta v. Bright Data](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/?p=26009), but it binds no one else and we shouldn't build on it |

**With their login (after a claim)**

- Instagram: the business/creator API with app review by Meta ([old API ended Dec 2024](https://smashballoon.com/instagram-is-shutting-down-basic-display-api-continue-displaying-instagram-feeds-on-your-site/)). Reads their posts and photos.
- Facebook Page events: needs Meta review; current status uncertain. Don't plan on it.
- TikTok: login plus app review; reads their videos.
- Shopify, Square: install a small app; reads the catalog with consent.
- Google Business Profile: owner login; reads their posts.

</details>

## Copyright and attribution

**Facts are free to use; words and photos are not. Before a claim, show only the facts we rewrite ourselves, with "from plantfoundry.com" and a link. Their photos and copy appear only after they claim and connect.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

- When, where, what kind and price are facts; copyright doesn't protect facts (*Feist v. Rural*, 1991).
- Photos and written copy are theirs. Today's venue reader already uses a venue's own listing photo; that is a risk worth one line from legal, or a switch to stock for unclaimed Pages.
- The safe-harbor rule for user uploads probably doesn't cover content we import ourselves, so pre-claim imports should stay facts-only.
- Google and Yelp both show unclaimed listings from data and offer a "claim this business" path. We match: Sample label, source link, an easy "fix or remove this" path (we have page removal requests), and a claim prompt.

</details>

## The agent: their content, our card, nobody's time

**One scheduled run a day, per unclaimed Page: read its machine-readable sources, write each dated thing as a card (kind · when · where, a short headline in our words, "from their site" link), label it Sample, drop duplicates and anything past, and retire the made-up samples once a real one exists. No human in the loop; a failure leaves the Page as it was.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

- **Find the feeds:** from the website on file, look for event data, a calendar link, a blog feed, a store feed, a YouTube channel. Most Pages need this once; re-check monthly.
- **Read and turn into cards:** event → Event card with day and time; a dated sale or product drop → Sale or Limited; a blog post → News only if it names a date and place, else skip. Headline rewritten from facts; never copied body text.
- **Label:** Sample, with "from {site}" and the link, until claimed. The same card turns into the business's own post, without the label, on claim.
- **Keep it clean:** one post per Page + date + title (the venue reader's rule); leave at the shelf life by kind; never more than the Page's real calendar holds.
- **Runs unattended:** daily, with a weekly line on Don's dashboard: Pages read, cards made, Pages with nothing to read (the gap list for outreach).
- **After a claim:** "Connect Instagram / YouTube / Shopify" on the claim screen. Their posts with a date become cards automatically; they can switch any source off.
- **Member-benefit test** ("if it helps the average person we will use it"): a local sees what's on this week at places they'd never check five sites for; the business gets found for nothing; nobody posts twice. It passes for facts; it fails if we pass off their photos and words as ours.
- **Plant Foundry today:** v1 would add its products and news only. Its workshops arrive only after it claims and connects Instagram, which is the honest pitch: "you're already here; connect once and your events show up."

</details>

## Your call: what v1 reads

**A. Facts from their own sites, before claim (recommended).** Widen the venue reader to every unclaimed Page: event data, calendar and blog feeds, YouTube, products where allowed. Sample until claimed. Cheapest; builds on working code; fills the empty shelves now.

**C. A plus a SocialUs inbox for newsletters.** Subscribe to each business's newsletter and pull dated facts. Reaches what feeds miss; more moving parts and a trust question ("you're reading my emails").

**Recommend A now, then B as its own scenario** once a first business claims. C waits until A's gap list shows newsletters are where the missing events live.
