# Founding circle: the first five people (2026-10-21)

**As of 2026-10-21** · Source: hand-written, Launch Coach · Path: well-worn · Maintained by: hand (the owner confirms on next edit)

**Bottom line: before beta grows, five people outside the team use SocialUs and each has one 20-minute conversation with the PM. Agents prepare everything; the PM only talks and listens.**

Precedents: Y Combinator, "do things that don't scale" ([Graham, 2013](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)); *The Mom Test* (Fitzpatrick): ask about their past behaviour, never pitch; Front Porch Forum started with one Vermont town and its founders knocking on doors ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_Porch_Forum)); Ostrom's commons principle that those affected by the rules help make them.

## Who

Mix of two kinds, all in the Sacramento beta area: 3 people who run something (a farm stand, a bakery, a market vendor, a class) and 2 neighbours who'd look for them. The dark-site pilot list is a source for the first kind.

## The five questions (no pitch)

1. Tell me about the last time you looked for something local. What happened?
2. Where do you hear about what's going on nearby? What's annoying about it?
3. (Business) How do new customers find you now? What have you tried that didn't work?
4. Here's SocialUs. Find one thing you'd care about. (Watch; don't help.)
5. Who else should I talk to?

## Logging

After each talk the PM texts Dispatch a voice note or three lines. An agent writes it below with no names: role, town, what they said, what surprised.
