# Rules review, 2026-10-25

**Every ruling was tested against the soul: 391 rulings became 331, 7 stances were reversed to "we do it for members, and they can opt out", and 23 of the 32 questions waiting on the PM were decided by the soul and defaults.** The PM's one job here: read the list below for wording only.

## The rules that remain

**Soul (what we believe), every version**
1. We assume the best of each other.
2. Everything we do benefits all of us, not a few.
3. You choose.
4. We build the system, then get out of the way.
5. It works for you.
6. Never extractive.

**Defaults, for anything the rules don't cover:** trust first; offer a choice instead of deciding for people; share the benefit; make it self-serve; pick the well-worn path.

**Principles under the soul**
- SocialUs works for the member; algorithms rank for the member, not the platform.
- Two sides: Find is the plain tool; Discover may learn what you like.
- Data serves the members it comes from: we collect what we can use, never sell it, opting out is one step; between members, private by default.
- In public, locality is coarse; precision stays private.
- Promotion earns its place by serving members: marked, on Discover only, surplus to members, can be turned off; Find is never reordered by payment.
- AI helps people and doesn't stand in for them; what an assistant knows belongs to the member.
- Never extractive rules out selling data, revenue that works better when a member is stuck, and venture capital.
- Local means money comes back to where it was made.
- The platform is the technology layer, never the bank.
- People, not shells: one word, member.
- Groups are joined, never assigned.
- The platform records facts; it never performs legal acts.
- Measure inside the app; a number tunes the app for members and is never raised for its own sake.

**Absolutes (unchanged except one):** content needs a report path before it goes live; member data stays where the member put it; production asks first; public words are drafts until approved; rulings are dated lines; newer wins; a check proves itself. *Narrowed:* the PM decides ideas, what's in each version, and words and look-and-feel; agents decide the rest by the soul and report.
