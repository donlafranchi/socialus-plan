## What fills the empty shelves

**Most shelves are filled mostly by Samples today. Three changes fill them for real: the post-once importer reading every Page's own site (most shelves), a free/paid field on posts (Free this week), and members posting sales and rallies (Garage sales, Who's got a rally?).**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

- An empty shelf is absent (F121), so a thin shelf costs nothing; it shows up the day its first post does.
- Keyword queries are a start. A shelf's query should move to tags as Pages get tagged: dog-friendly, free, outdoors, kids.
- "Meet up with folks" and "Big nights out" overlap with venue shows; the one-show-per-screen rule handles it, and the first one down the page takes the item.

</details>

## A new name for "Wonder"

**Recommend *Rally*. It says people coming together behind something, reads well as a verb and a noun, and keeps "Count me in" as the button you already drafted. The name has many unrelated owners; the nearest is a bus app that confirms a trip once enough people sign up, so it needs a legal check before launch.**

<details><summary>Eight names, how each reads, and conflicts</summary>

| Name | Why | Post it | Progress | Shelf | Conflicts found |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rally** (recommended) | gathering people behind a plan; active, warm, American | "Start a rally" | "12 of 30 in" | "Who's got a rally?" | Common word with many owners. Closest: [Rally](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rally-rider/id1436524445), group bus trips confirmed once enough sign up; [Rally.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rally.org), fundraising. A political or car-rally echo. |
| **Hunch** | closest in spirit to "wonder": an idea you test with others | "Post a hunch" | "12 of 30 in" | "Who's got a hunch?" | Hunch friend-finding and dating apps; old Hunch.com (closed). Doesn't say "join". |
| **Count me in** | your current draft kind name; perfect as a button | "Post a Count me in" (awkward) | "12 of 30 counted in" | "Count me in" | Several live COUNT ME IN registrations, one in software, one in education/events ([list](https://markinton.com/trademark/count-me-in-88947811)). Keep it as a button, not a brand. |
| **Who's in?** | the exact question being asked | "Ask who's in" | "12 of 30 in" | "Who's in?" | [Who's in!?](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/whos-in/id997696703) plans app; a [WhatsApp RSVP tool](https://www.producthunt.com/products/who-s-in); the old trademark is dead. |
| **Let's** | a sentence starter: "Let's start a run club" | "Post a Let's" | "12 of 30 in" | "Who's got a Let's?" | Crowded: [Let's Hang!](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lets-hang/id1547911833), Let's Gather!. Odd as a noun. |
| **Huddle** | a small group getting together to plan | "Start a huddle" | "12 of 30 in the huddle" | "Who's huddling?" | Slack Huddles; Huddle (work software). Sounds private. |
| **Tally** | the progress bar is the whole idea | "Start a tally" | "12 of 30 tallied" | "Tallies near you" | [Tally](https://tally.so) form builder. Feels like counting, not doing. |
| **Spark** | the first spark that gets something going | "Post a spark" | "12 of 30 in" | "Who's got a spark?" | Very crowded: Spark Driver, Spark Mail, Adobe Spark. |
