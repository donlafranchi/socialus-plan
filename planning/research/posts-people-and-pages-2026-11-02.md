# Posts from people and posts from Pages

**As of 2026-11-02** · Source: hand-written · Maintained by: hand (set by tend-docs from the last commit date; the owner confirms on next edit)

**Bottom line for Don:** Facebook, Nextdoor and Google all split posts the way you're thinking: **a Page speaks for an organization; a person speaks for themselves**, and the two look different on the card. Today every SocialUs post needs a Page, so a yard sale, a lost dog or a free couch has nowhere to go. **Recommend B: let a person post a small set of neighbour kinds (Lost & found, Free, For sale, Ask for a recommendation, Happening) without a Page, signed with display name and neighbourhood only, each one expiring on its own.** Proposals stay where you parked them.

**Your call:** A, B or C (section 5). I recommend **B, starting with three kinds: Lost & found, Free / for sale, and Ask a neighbour.**

## The picture
**Two speakers, one feed: a Page speaks as an organization and lasts; a person speaks as a neighbour and their post fades. Kinds decide which rows each lands in.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

```
Page ──► Page post   (Update · Offer · Event · Limited · Reminder · Hiring · Proposal)
           shows: Page name and logo, the Page's address, Follow, Save, RSVP
Person ─► Person post (Lost & found · Free / for sale · Ask a neighbour · Happening)
           shows: display name, avatar, neighbourhood; Reply, Save; no profile to tap
Proposal ── parked (can it stand without a Page?), unchanged by this doc
```

You already pointed this way on 2026-11-01: the draft **Locals tip** ("a tip is a person speaking", recommendations and happenings, never a review) and your 11-01 note that a post "either belongs to a page or a person without a page". This doc makes that a concrete choice.

## 1. How Facebook does posts
**One post shape, three places to post from. A person posts to friends (they pick the audience), a Page posts to the public, and a Group post shows the person and the Group together. The feed mixes all three, ranked, plus suggestions from strangers.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

**Where a post comes from**
- **A person's profile.** The author picks an audience on every post: Public, Friends, Friends except…, Specific friends, Only me. Shown with their real name and photo; tapping the name opens their profile. ([Facebook Help: audience](https://www.facebook.com/help/211513702214269))
- **A Page.** Always public, shown with the Page's name and logo, not the person behind it. The people running it are hidden behind the Page. Pages get insights, ads, a call-to-action button. ([Facebook Help: Pages](https://www.facebook.com/help/282489752085908))
- **A Group.** Shown as "Person ▸ Group". Who sees it follows the Group's privacy (public or private). Admins approve members and can approve posts first. Neighbourhood buy-sell groups live here.

**What a post carries:** author and photo, time ("3h"), the audience icon, text cut off with "See more" after a few lines, photos or video, a link preview; then a reaction count (Like, Love, Care, Haha, Wow, Sad, Angry), comment count and share count; then React · Comment · Share.

**How the feed mixes them:** one ranked Feed of friends, Pages and Groups you joined, plus "Suggested for you" from accounts you don't follow. There's a separate chronological Feeds tab for those who want it. ([Meta: how Feed works](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-feed/))

**Special post types:**
- **Events** can be made by a person, a Page or a Group; they have their own page with Going / Interested and land in an Events tab.
- **Marketplace** listings are made by people (personal profiles), not Pages; price, photos, rough location, Message seller. They live in their own tab, and some also show in buy-sell Groups. Businesses sell through Shops instead. ([Facebook Help: Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/1889067784738765))

</details>

<details><summary>How others split people and organizations</summary>

| App | People post | Organizations post | How the two differ |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Nextdoor**](https://help.nextdoor.com/) | To their neighbourhood, after an address check. Kinds: General, For sale & free, Lost & found, Recommendations, Events, Safety | Business Pages post updates and paid deals | Person shows name and neighbourhood; business shows its Page. The "Crime & safety" threads are where it went bad: Nextdoor added a slow-down form for safety posts (2016) and a "kindness reminder" before hurtful replies (2019) |
| [**Instagram**](https://help.instagram.com/502981923235522) | Personal accounts | Business / creator accounts | Same post; the business gets contact buttons and stats. No difference in kind |
| [**Yelp**](https://www.yelp.com/support) | Reviews and photos about a business | Business replies and paid "Yelp Connect" updates | People talk about places; places talk to people |
| [**Meetup**](https://help.meetup.com/) | Members comment and join | Only a Group's organizers post Events | No person posts at all outside a Group |
| [**Google Business Profile**](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169) | Reviews, photos, Local Guides | Business posts Updates, Offers, Events | The model for our Update · Offer · Event words (11-01) |

**The pattern:** where people post on their own (Facebook, Nextdoor), it is tied to a real identity and a rough place, and it's sorted by kind (for sale, lost, recommendation). Where it's an organization, the post is signed by the organization and lives on its Page. Nobody lets a person post as if they were an organization.

</details>

## 2. What we have today
**Every post belongs to a Page. Only the people who run a Page can post, in seven kinds. The rules around people are strict: no public profile, display name and avatar only, phone check before any content, and members fix problems first before the operator does.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

- **The app today:** "Posts, events and proposals live on a Page. Start one first." In the database a post cannot exist without its Page.
- **Kinds** (words drafts, Google-style per 11-01): Event, Update, Offer, Limited, Reminder, Hiring, Proposal. Picked first in the composer. Each has its own shelf life; undated posts leave Explore after 14 days.
- **Who can post:** whoever runs the Page. Since 11-01 the ➕ offers only "Start a Page" and "Propose something"; Post and Event moved onto the Page.

**Rulings that bear on person posts:**
- **"The launch is a yellow pages of organizations, not a white pages of people"** (2026-10-01). Person posts must not turn into a directory of people.
- **No person-nouns** in the app: never "seller", "neighbour-vendor" or similar labels on a person.
- **Member privacy:** no public member profile or link (10-01, 10-06); others see a display name and avatar, never a legal name (09-30); a stranger can read no member field (09-30); a member's home area is private and never a target (09-21); "anything similar to stalking needs to be reduced" (09-14).
- **Location:** a Page's address is public if given, with a neighbourhood as the safer option (09-09, 09-30). Nothing yet for a person's post.
- **Phone check before any content, [always]** (10-31): "we want phone numbers before anyone can post any kind of content." It already names a "non-Page post".
- **Self-correct first** (10-11): with one operator, problems go back to the people involved before staff step in. Reports start with a courtesy notice.
- **Proposals without a Page: parked** (11-01). You're still thinking about whether a Proposal is its own organizing thing. This doc doesn't touch it.
- **Locals tip, draft** (11-01): a person's recommendation or happening, "not a Post", never a review, mixed in Explore with a filter to split them.

</details>

## 3. Posts from people without a Page
**Five real local needs have no home today. Lost pets, free stuff, one-off sales and "who knows a good…" fit locals supporting locals and pass the value test. A yard sale and selling one item are borderline: a person post, not a Page. Repeat sellers should be nudged to a Page.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

| Need | Locals supporting locals? | Value test (only locals know · gets people together · stays fresh · operator load) | Person post or light Page? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lost or found pet** | Strongly | Only locals · urgent, expires · near-zero upkeep | **Person post.** Nobody makes a Page for a lost dog |
| **Free couch / curb alert** | Yes, keeps things local | Only locals · gone in days | **Person post** |
| **Ask for a recommendation** ("good plumber?") | Yes, sends people to local Pages | Answers point to Pages, which grows the yellow pages | **Person post**, and the answers should link Pages. This is your Locals tip |
| **Garage / yard sale** | Yes, gets people out on a Saturday | Dated · gets people together | **Person post with a date** |
| **Selling one item** | Mild; it's Marketplace's turf | Lives somewhere better (Facebook Marketplace) | **Person post**, capped; a third sale in a month suggests "Start a Page" |
| **One-off "who wants to…"** | Yes | This is a Proposal | **Parked** with the Proposal question |

**Risks and how the precedents handle them**
- **Spam.** The phone check already covers it (one real mobile per account). Add a small cap: a few active person posts at once, each kind with its own expiry (7 days lost/free, 14 sale). Craigslist and Nextdoor both limit by kind.
- **Privacy (name and where they live).** Show display name, avatar and **neighbourhood only**, never a street, never a tappable profile. A yard sale's street address is the author's choice on that one post, hidden after the date. Fits "stalking reduced" and "no public profile".
- **Moderation load for one operator.** Short life is the main defence: most person posts are gone within two weeks. Reports use the courtesy-first path already built. No comment threads at launch, only "Reply privately" once messaging exists (or "I'm interested" / "I saw it"), which removes the place where fights happen.
- **Turning into Nextdoor's complaint threads.** Nextdoor's worst problems came from open "Crime & safety" posts and public comment threads. **No complaint, safety or general-chat kind; only the fixed kinds; no public comments.** Your 11-01 "never a review" already points this way.

</details>

## 4. Two categories, how they'd look
**A Page's post is signed by the Page and leads to the Page. A person's post is signed with a display name and neighbourhood, leads nowhere, and fades. They sit in different rows, never the same one.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

| | Page post | Person post |
|---|---|---|
| **Signed by** | Page name and logo | Display name, avatar, "in Midtown" |
| **Lead line** | EVENT · SAT 5–8 PM · MIDTOWN (as built) | LOST · 2 DAYS AGO · MIDTOWN |
| **Tap the author** | Opens the Page | Nothing (no public profile) |
| **Do with it** | Save, Follow the Page, RSVP, Count me in | Save, "I'm interested" / "I saw it"; reply once messaging exists |
| **Lives** | By kind; undated ones 14 days, then "Earlier" on the Page | 7–14 days, then gone from everyone but the author |
| **Where it shows** | Explore rows, search, its Page | Explore rows of its own ("Neighbours: lost & found", "Free nearby"), search, the author's own You |

**Keeping Pages and posts apart (ruled 11-01, web #1154).** Rows already hold one type each. Person posts get their own rows by kind, so a row is never half Pages, half people. In search they're one more labelled group ("From neighbours") next to "Places & groups" and the post kinds.

**One thing to square:** your 11-01 Locals tip note says tips show "mixed with Pages' posts", with a search filter to split them. Separate rows fit your later rows ruling better; the search filter still works. Flagged, not decided.

</details>

## 5. Options
**B lets neighbours post the few things that have no home, with the least new risk. A keeps us pure but leaves lost pets and yard sales out. C is Nextdoor, which is what we don't want to become.**

<details><summary>Details</summary>

- **A. Pages only, as now.** Simplest, no new privacy or moderation load. But real everyday needs go to Facebook and Nextdoor, and a "lightweight Page for a lost dog" is clumsy.
- **B. Person posts in a few fixed kinds** (recommended). Display name and neighbourhood, short life, no public comments, phone check first, own rows. Adds a second speaker without a people directory.
- **C. Open person posts** (any text, comments, like a Facebook profile or Nextdoor). Most activity, but it's a white pages of people, and one operator can't moderate open threads.

**Recommendation: B.** Smallest first version:
1. Three kinds: **Lost & found**, **Free / for sale** (one item or a yard sale with a date), **Ask a neighbour** (the Locals tip: recommendations and happenings).
2. ➕ gets a third choice beside "Start a Page" and "Propose something".
3. Signed with display name, avatar, neighbourhood; street address optional on a yard sale only.
4. Expires on its own (7 or 14 days); a small cap on active posts.
5. No comments; "I'm interested" / "I saw it" only, until messaging exists.
6. Own rows in Explore; one labelled group in search.

**What it leaves alone:** whether a Proposal can stand without a Page (parked); the final words (all drafts for you); a public person profile (stays ruled out).

</details>
