# Direction for builders (2026-10-10)

Internal and early. No public commitment, copy, absolute or legal wording. There are no users yet, so no detail is needed. The paragraph itself lives in `product/foundation/what-this-is.md` § Direction, with a pointer from `CLAUDE.md`.

- We're not taking money from traditional investors. We'd rather be owned by our members; how that works is open.
- We don't want a few people getting rich off our members.
- We may use the same tools other platforms use, aimed at the people they come from.

**Question while building:** when a feature collects or uses member data, whose is it, and who gains? If the answer is someone else, or only us, check with Don. It is a prompt, not a gate.

**Name, if one is wanted:** "Reclaim" (a verb; the tools are the same, the aim differs). Alternatives: "Common Use", "Same Tools, Our Side". Internal only.

**Old docs that read as flat refusals of a tool** (`monetization.md` § Refused outright, `model.md` § Personalized means interests, `metrics.md`, the ROADMAP Won't list) are now judgment calls to raise with Don, not settled yeses. The first voice pass (`copy-voice-pass-2026-10-10.md`) says nothing public about data use.
