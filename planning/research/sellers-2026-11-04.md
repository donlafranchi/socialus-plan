# What SocialUs does for people who make, sell and run things

**As of 2026-11-04** · Source: hand-written · Maintained by: hand

## Bottom line for Don

**What we have today:**
- Anyone can make a Page for free and post seven kinds of thing.
- People can find a Page in search and on Explore's shelves without the owner paying, and nothing is sold in the app, so we take nothing.

**What's missing:**
- Owners don't own their audience yet. They see a count of the people who get updates, not names, and they can't send those people a note.
- Search engines barely see our Pages.

**The plan:**
- Fix those two gaps first.
- Add video as tap-to-play links to the owner's own TikTok or YouTube.
- Rewrite /join so it claims only what ships.

- **Video:**
  - **A:** tap-to-play TikTok and YouTube on a Page, with nothing loading until someone taps. About 14 hours.
  - **B (cheaper):** a "Watch on TikTok" style link with the owner's own still picture. About 4 hours.
  - **Skip:**
    - Uploading video to SocialUs (it needs video moderation we don't have).
    - Instagram embeds (Meta app review, business verification, and no thumbnails since 2025-11-03).
    - Posting out to those platforms (audits and sign-in flows, weeks each).
- **Top 5 gaps, best value for the effort:**
  1. A link on each post (3 h).
  2. Search engines can read Pages and events (10 h).
  3. Owners see who gets updates by name and can send them a note (20 h).
  4. "Where I'll be this week" for market stalls (8 h).
  5. Private counts for owners (10 h).
- **The /join page is wrong today.**
  - It says "vendor", which our voice rules ban.
  - It uses an em dash.
  - It promises things that don't ship: being on the map and searchable by product the moment you're live, and updates about market days.
  - The new copy is in the brief, with every claim marked shipped. I left out everything coming.
- **Your calls:**
  1. Approve the /join copy.
  2. Video A or B.
  3. Paid promotion on Discover. It's allowed by the 2026-10-25 ruling, but it is the very pay-to-play that drives people off the big platforms. Keep it, or rule it out for Pages?
  4. The transaction fee: a rate and a start date, so the page can state it.
- **To make room before soft launch, these leave scope:**
  - payments;
  - messages;
  - full product listings;
  - video upload;
  - posting out to other platforms;
  - opening "Open your town".
- **Next:** you approve the copy and pick A or B. Then a Code session builds /join and gaps 1 and 2.

Research only. Nothing here is a ruling, and nothing gets built until you approve the copy.

## 2. How small businesses and makers get found today

**Getting found on the big platforms now means paying for it, or putting up with rules that change under you, with no one to call. Fact: free reach on Facebook is about 1.6% of followers. Fact: Google clicks fall by about half when an AI summary appears. Fact: account lockouts are bad enough that 41 attorneys general wrote to Meta.** The pain is real and documented. The fixes people trust are the ones they own: an email list and their own site.

<details><summary>Google</summary>

**Fact:**
- Local results rank on relevance, distance and prominence. Reviews help, and you can't pay for a better local ranking ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).
- Ads cost about $5.26 a click on average, up about 13% in a year; arts and entertainment run about $1.60 ([LocalIQ benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/), vendor data).
- When an AI summary appears, people click a result on 8% of visits instead of 15% ([Pew, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)).
- Organic click rate on informational searches with AI Overviews fell 61% ([Seer, 2025](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update), vendor data).
- Free Business Profile websites shut down in 2024 ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/websites-created-with-google-business-profiles-to-shut-down-in-march/509794/)), and chat was removed in July 2024 ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-business-profile-chat-is-gone-and-call-history-going-away-444199)).
- Suspension appeals can be submitted once, and a restricted account can't claim another profile ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4569145)).
- Fake reviews have been banned by the FTC since October 2024 ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)).

**Opinion:** forums and reinstatement vendors report a wave of suspensions and video re-verification in 2025, with two to four weeks to get reinstated ([SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-business-profiles-reverification-39712.html)).

</details>

<details><summary>Facebook and Instagram</summary>

**Fact:**
- Average reach in 2025 was 1.65% of followers on Facebook and 3.5% on Instagram, with Instagram down about 12% in a year ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/), vendor data).
- Meta has steadily moved the feed to recommended posts from accounts people don't follow, and reports time-spent gains from it each quarter ([Fortune, 2022](https://fortune.com/2022/07/28/mark-zuckerberg-instagram-double-ai-recommended-content-2023)).
- New Reels go first to people who don't follow you ([Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through)).
- In December 2025 Facebook tested limiting Pages without paid verification to links in 2 posts a month ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages)).
- The Marketplace fee on shipped sales doubled to 10% in April 2024; local pickup is free ([press](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/facebook-marketplace-doubling-selling-fees/)).
- 41 attorneys general told Meta in 2024 they "refuse to operate as the customer service representatives" for locked-out users ([NAAG letter](https://www.naag.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Policy-Letter-to-Meta-on-Account-Takeover-2024-03-06.pdf)).
- Direct support now comes with paid Meta Verified, roughly $15 to $500 a month ([Meta](https://about.meta.com/technologies/meta-verified)).
- Facebook is testing a Local tab that brings Marketplace, Groups and Events together ([Engadget, 2024](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/facebook-is-pushing-local-content-and-events-to-try-to-win-back-young-adults-161742961.html)).

**Opinion:**
- Facebook Events has been "deprioritized" (a ticketing vendor; not confirmed).
- You can't export your followers' contact details from a Page; I found no Meta page confirming this.

</details>

<details><summary>Marketplaces, and what people say they need</summary>

**Fees, for context:**
- **Etsy:** a $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% per sale, and about 3% plus $0.25 for payment processing. Offsite ads take 12–15% ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees)).
- **Amazon Handmade:** 15% per sale ([Amazon](https://sell.amazon.com/programs/handmade)).
- **Eventbrite:** at the door, $1 plus 2.9% per ticket ([Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/755615/)).

**What surveys say (fact):**
- 63% of new small businesses rely on social media as their main channel ([Constant Contact, 2025](https://news.constantcontact.com/2025-03-04-New-Wave-of-SMBs-is-Social-First,-Technology-Driven,-and-Highly-Adaptable)).
- 44% call email their most effective channel, up from 23% ([Small Biz Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/constant-contact-smb-trends-2025/), secondary).
- 54% struggle to make enough content ([Verizon via eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/smbs-struggle-keep-up-with-social-media-evolution)).

**Not verified:** Etsy's rule that offsite ads become mandatory over $10k, boosted-post costs, and the Ahrefs click figure.

</details>

## 3. Where we can give people more say

**The biggest lever is the audience. People who get updates from a Page should be people the owner can see and reach, inside SocialUs, without paying. Next is being findable outside SocialUs, then never charging for reach.** Each fits the Charter: "a Page costs nothing to create", "no member can pay to rank above another", and wealth stays with the people who earned it.

<details><summary>Pain by pain</summary>

| Pain | What we do now | What we could do | Effort | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **No ownership of the audience** | Get updates and alerts work; the owner sees a count | Show the owner who gets updates, by name (F067 already rules this). Let them send one note to all of them in the app (#744). Members keep their email private; no export of member contact details | ~20 h | Strong. Earned attention is the product working (2026-09-12) |
| **Pay-to-play reach** | Search is never reordered by money. Shelves rotate so different owners lead. Nothing is charged | Keep it that way for Pages. Paid promotion on Discover is allowed by ruling (2026-10-25); your call | 0 h | Strong, unless promotion is kept |
| **Changing rules** | Shelf and alert rules are written down and ruled | Plain notes to owners when what decides placement changes | ~3 h per change | Good |
| **Lockouts, no one to call** | Sign-in by email code; reports go to the poster first and a person decides | A plain "contact a person" line for owners | ~2 h | Good |
| **Local reach beyond the app** | Share previews only | Sitemap, structured data for Pages and events (LocalBusiness, Event), and public post text visible to search engines (#743, #724) | ~10 h | Strong: found locally without ads |
| **Fees** | None; no commerce in the app | State the rate before payments start | Copy only | Ruled: income comes, fees follow success |
| **Too much to post everywhere** | Pages started from the owner's own site; weekly repeats | Read their own calendar or site again on a schedule (F090, F096) | ~16 h | Good (post once, option A) |

**Flags for "wealth circulates, never extracted":**
- **Exporting member emails to owners would take members' data.** Keep it out; the note goes through SocialUs.
- **Paid promotion** is allowed and marked, but it's the exact thing people are leaving. Your call.
- **Hearts show the owner the names.** That's ruled (#269). The landing copy says so plainly, so no one is surprised.

</details>

## 4. Video

**A: tap-to-play TikTok and YouTube on the owner's Page. B: a plain link with the owner's own picture. Skip upload, Instagram embeds and posting out.** We pick A or B on cost and risk. Both keep video off any feed, send no member data anywhere before a tap, and point people outward.

<details><summary>Options, terms, cost and risk</summary>

| | What it is | Terms and APIs | Cost | Risk | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Tap-to-play embeds** (recommended) | The owner pastes up to 3 TikTok or YouTube links. Each shows the owner's own still picture with "Plays from TikTok". Only a tap loads the player: TikTok's [Embed Player](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/embed-player) with only their own videos suggested, YouTube's [privacy-enhanced mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780) | No key or app review for either. TikTok's old embed script is being retired | $0 | Low. Before a tap, nothing third-party loads. After a tap the platform sets its own storage, so we say where it plays from | ~14 h |
| **B. Link out** (cheaper) | "Watch on TikTok" (or Instagram or YouTube) under the owner's picture, using the social links Pages already have | None | $0 | None | ~4 h |
| Skip: Instagram embeds | Needs a Meta app, [App Review and Business Verification](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/features-reference/oembed-read); thumbnails removed 2025-11-03 | Heavy | $0 | Meta's script tracks | ~20 h plus review |
| Skip: upload to SocialUs | Hosting is cheap ([Mux](https://www.mux.com/pricing) is about $0 at 500 Pages × 3 clips; [bunny](https://bunny.net/pricing/stream/) about $1) | Must report CSAM on knowledge ([18 U.S.C. §2258A](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A)); image scanning tools don't clearly cover video | ~$0–15/month | **High:** video moderation we can't staff yet | ~30 h |
| Skip: posting out | TikTok's [Content Posting API](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines) is private-only until audited. Instagram needs App Review. YouTube got easier ([revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/revision_history): unverified projects no longer forced private as of 2026-11-02) | Weeks of review each | $0 | Tokens for owners' accounts | ~40 h+ |

**Guardrails for A:**
- At most 3 clips, on the Page only.
- Never on Explore shelves.
- No autoplay.
- No "next video".
- The button under each clip points out to the owner's next event or post.

</details>
