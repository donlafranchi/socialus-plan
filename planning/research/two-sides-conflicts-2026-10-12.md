# Two sides: what conflicts

Don, 2026-10-12: the app has two sides, "the tool" (finding something specific, like Yelp) and "the fun" (exploring with nothing in mind, where an algorithm may serve what interests the member, more like Instagram or TikTok). It is the newer direction, after beta, not ready for council (`IMAGINE.md`, `PRINCIPLES.md`). Anything earlier that goes against it is reviewed and updated. Each line below is tagged `[for now]` and scoped to the tool side; inline in the doc, or in its frontmatter (`under-review`) where the doc is over its size budget.

1. `product/charter.md`, flip 1: "never by what holds your attention"; "a place, time and relationship engine, not a watch-time optimizer. Browse is complete, not ranked by your interests". The Charter text is not rewritten; the tags are lost on the next `regen-charter` until the principle is reflected there.
2. `planning/scenario-F059.md`: Not this ("Ranking by what holds attention", "Personal content as a private feed") and the 2026-09-12 Home/Browse ruling.
3. `planning/scenario-F096.md`: "the member works with the platform, not an algorithm".
4. `planning/scenario-F098.md` criterion 4: no input measures time spent, dwell, scroll depth or opens.
5. `product/ui/surfaces.md`: Browse is complete and not ranked by the member's interests.
6. `product/foundation/model.md`: Browse not ranked by interests; "Never: what keeps someone scrolling"; "not licence for a feed algorithm".
7. `product/systems/discovery.md`: not a watch-time optimizer; the anti-patterns (watch-time, dwell-time, honest stop card); a view never counts as interaction.
8. `product/foundation/metrics.md` and `what-this-is.md`: no engagement or attention measures; no engagement-optimized ranking.
9. `product/foundation/verbs.md`: the "not-addicting constraint".
10. `product/systems/groups.md`: no engagement-ranked Group discovery feed.
11. `product/exploration/search-direction.md`: ranking uses no attention.
12. `product/foundation/monetization.md`: no attention-capture advertising.
