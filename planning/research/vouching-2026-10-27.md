# Vouching: a member posts about a place they love

**As of 2026-10-27** · Source: hand-written · Maintained by: hand (set by tend-docs from the last commit date; the owner confirms on next edit)

**An idea to explore, not a plan.** A member with no Page of their own posts about a business they go to, framed as vouching for the owner rather than scoring them. The entry is in IMAGINE; this file holds its precedents and open questions.

*Don, 2026-10-27, verbatim:* "this app is local discovery, but it's also people vouching for other people so if I frequent of business and I vouched for that business owner, I'd like to be able to create a post or an announcement or something that applies to that business and this is kind of like a review in that I'm vouching for them and we don't have a way to do that right now without a person having their own page … these are timely things like this place is having a sale or you know I had the best time experience food whatever at this at this place or this place is the best place for a date night."

**What it is:** a member with no Page of their own posts about a business they go to. It's timely ("they're having a sale", "best meal I've had this month") and review-like, but framed as vouching for the owner: a recommendation, not a score.

**Folded in here:** the 2026-09-09 one-liner **Vetting & vouching**, a community trust signal for producer claims, separate from a behaviour rating.

*Analysis, agent:* this is the obstacle named under "Discovery that knows what a person is optimizing for": nobody can say anything about a Page they don't own.

**Precedents:**

| App | What a member can do |
|---|---|
| [Nextdoor Faves](https://blog.nextdoor.com/small-business-improvements) | Verified neighbours "Fave" a business, and posts that mention it collect on its page |
| Google and Yelp reviews | Star reviews. [The owner can't remove one, only report it](https://www.vendasta.com/blog/how-to-delete-google-reviews/), and lumping every reviewer together is what Don disliked on 2026-09-19 |
| [Beli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beli_(app)) | Ranks places against each other and shows the average of your friends' scores |
| [Instagram tags and collabs](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/how-to-use-the-instagram-collab-feature-for-posts-and-reels/) | A photo tag files the post under the business's Tagged tab; a collab needs the business to accept, then shows on both profiles |

**Open questions:**
- Does a vouch show on the business's Page, or only in Explore and on the member's profile?
- Can the owner hide one, accept it (as with Instagram collabs), or only report it (as with Google)?
- What's the shelf life? Is a "sale" vouch dated like a post while "best date night" lasts?
- Is it a statement or a score? (No stars, if it's vouching.)
- Who may vouch: anyone, or members with a real tie such as a follow, a check-in or a purchase?
- How do we keep it honest: paid vouches, owners vouching for themselves, rivals?

**What would make this real:** a ruling that members may publish statements about Pages they don't own, and enough members in one metro that a vouch reaches someone.
