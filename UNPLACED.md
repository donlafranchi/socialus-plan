# UNPLACED.md

The junk drawer: things with no home yet. One dated line each, ending `[unplaced raised=YYYY-MM-DD owner=planning|code]`. **Each item leaves within 14 days** to its home (`ops-pattern/process/WHERE-THINGS-GO.md`), an open-question marker, `IMAGINE.md`, an Issue, or is deleted, and its line is removed, never copied. The lint fails on an item older than 14 days or more than 20 items; the handoff drains it. Loose files go to `~/Projects/agent-temp`, not here.
